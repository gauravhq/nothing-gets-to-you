It helps to know who is doing the choosing. When something gets to you, it can feel like two of you are fighting: the hot one who wants to snap, and the cool one who knows better and shows up late. The cool one usually loses, because the hot one has already spoken.

There is a third. Quieter than both. The part of you that can watch the other two, that notices the heat climbing, that is reading this line right now and knows it is reading. These are not three separate people in your head, just three names for one mind doing three things.

Calm people have not killed their anger. They feel the same heat you do. They have learned to stand half a step back from it, watch it, and let it pass. That half step is the third option. [Calm is not numb](/blog/calm-is-not-numb/).
