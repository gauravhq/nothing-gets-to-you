Part of the skill is not built in the hot moment at all. It is built upstream. You are far more reactive when you are depleted. The same comment that rolls off you on a good day can overwhelm you on a bad one. So a surprising amount of calm comes from plain maintenance: enough sleep, enough stillness, a little time that is not spoken for. That is not indulgence. It is the ground the whole skill stands on.

And the progress is easy to miss, because it shows up in the negative, in things that stop happening. A whole week goes by without the email feud that used to eat your Thursdays. An argument at home simply does not start. You sleep through a night that a year ago you would have spent replaying something until three.

Those quiet non-events are the real milestones. [You cannot pour from an empty cup](/blog/you-cannot-pour-from-an-empty-cup/).
