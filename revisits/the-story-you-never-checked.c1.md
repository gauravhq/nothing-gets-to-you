There is a second story worth checking, and it is about you, not them. Sometimes what looks like a trigger is just the last straw on a load you were already carrying.

You snap at your partner over something small, and it feels like the small thing caused it. Look closer. You slept badly. Work has been grinding on you all week. You skipped lunch. You walked into the room already full to the brim, and their comment was the drop that made the glass spill over. The comment took the blame.

On days like that, carry yourself the way you would carry a glass filled to the very edge. Tell the people close to you, honestly: I am running on empty today. It is not you. And be slower to trust the size of your own reactions, because on a full day everything feels bigger than it really is. [Sometimes it is not the trigger](/blog/sometimes-its-not-the-trigger/).
