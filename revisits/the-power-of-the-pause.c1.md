Some arguments happen out loud, face to face, and there is no drafts folder for your mouth. The pause still works there. It just has to be smaller.

Breathe out a little longer than you breathe in. Nobody in the room will know you did it, and for some people it buys one clear second. Or ask a real question instead of firing back: what do you mean by that? It hands you a few seconds you did not have, and it can help the other person feel heard.

You are also allowed not to have the perfect reply ready. "I do not want to answer this badly. Give me a second." That is the pause, out loud, with no loss of face. And if the heat is simply too high: "I want to finish this, but not like this. Let me take ten minutes." A break is not losing the argument. [The spoken pause](/blog/the-spoken-pause/).
