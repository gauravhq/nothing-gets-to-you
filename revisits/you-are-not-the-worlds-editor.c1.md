One question takes most of the pressure off this job. Think about your own real changes of heart, the ones that actually stuck. Did any of them come from losing an argument? From someone reciting facts at you in a smug tone?

Almost certainly not. They came slowly, from something you went through, from a person you came to love who lived another way, from your own mind turning something over long after the conversation ended. That is how it works for the person you are itching to fix, too.

And corrections have a cost even when they are right. Picture a senior colleague who corrects every small thing a junior says, in front of the team. Each correction is probably right. Over a year, the junior stops offering ideas at all. The senior won every point and taught the team to go quiet. [Correcting pushes people away](/blog/correcting-pushes-people-away/).
