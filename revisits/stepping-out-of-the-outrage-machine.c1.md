The machine is not just strangers. Some of the hardest moments happen in chats with people you know, and those are trickier, because you cannot just scroll past. You will see these people on Monday, or at the next family lunch.

In a group chat the audience is real, small, and known, so the pull is stronger. You do not just want to be right. You want to be seen being right, by these particular people. And a sharp message at ten at night does not vanish into a river of strangers. It stays. It gets screenshotted. It becomes the thing everyone quietly remembers at the next gathering.

So if something truly needs saying to one person, say it to that person, not to the whole group, where it becomes a performance and a permanent record. [The family thread](/blog/the-family-thread/).
