Here is the difference in its smallest form: the same disagreement, opened two ways.

The reaction: "You are wrong. How can you even think that?" It attacks the person and turns a difference of opinion into a fight about who is stupid.

The response: "That is interesting. I see it pretty differently. Can I tell you how it looks from where I stand?" It keeps the door open and leaves the other person their dignity. You have not given up your view. You have offered it in a way they can actually receive.

The opening often sets the direction. Start with an accusation and the other person's guard goes up in the first few seconds. So spend your care at the very start. You are not choosing whether to be honest. You are choosing whether to open a door or a battle. [You set the tone](/blog/you-set-the-tone/).
