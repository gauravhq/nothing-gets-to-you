---
title: "The scoreboard nobody else sees"
date: 2027-12-19
pageDescription: "You keep a running tally of who was right. You are the only one looking at it."
---

There is a scoreboard you carry into every disagreement. Points for when you were right. Points against for when you were wronged. You track it carefully.

Here is the thing. You are the only one looking at it. The other person is not keeping your score. Half the time they have forgotten the argument you are still tallying. That scoreboard exists in one head, and it is yours.

And it does not make you happier. It keeps old grievances warm, ready to be pulled out. It turns people into opponents. It makes you count instead of connect.

You can put the scoreboard down. Not because you were wrong, but because keeping score was never bringing you anything except a reason to stay tense. Nobody is checking your total. You are free to stop counting whenever you like.
