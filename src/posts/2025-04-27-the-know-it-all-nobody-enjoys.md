title: "The know-it-all nobody enjoys"
date: 2025-04-27
pageDescription: "The person who corrects everything usually has no idea how they land. Check, honestly, that it is not you."
---

We all know one. The person who has to correct everything, add the fact, top the point, be right about every detail. And we all know how it feels to be around them. Tiring. A little tense. Something to endure.

Here is the uncomfortable part. That person usually does not know that is how they land. They think they are being helpful, sharing knowledge, keeping things accurate. Meanwhile everyone around them is quietly bracing.

It is worth checking, honestly, whether you ever slip into it. The constant correcting, the need to have the last accurate word, comes from a good place but it costs you warmth.

The fix is not to go silent. It is to let far more slide. To hold the correction, skip the extra fact, let someone else be right. People do not warm to the one who is always right. They warm to the one who is easy to be around, which is a much better thing to be.
