---
title: "You are not the world's editor"
date: 2022-11-20
pageDescription: "The job of correcting everyone is unpaid, endless, and impossible. You can quit."
---

Somewhere along the way, many of us took on a job we never applied for: correcting everyone. The wrong opinion in the group chat. The friend who has their facts slightly off. The stranger online who is, obviously, mistaken.

It is unpaid. It never ends. And it is impossible, because the supply of wrong things is infinite.

Letting it go is not the same as not caring. It is deciding that your peace is worth more than being the one who set the record straight on something that did not matter.

Try one small experiment this week. Pick a moment where you would normally jump in to correct something harmless, and just do not. Notice, afterward, that nothing fell apart. The world kept turning. And your evening stayed your own.

Save your voice for the things that actually matter. There are fewer of them than the reactive mind assumes.
