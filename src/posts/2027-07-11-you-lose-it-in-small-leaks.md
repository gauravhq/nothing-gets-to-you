---
title: "You lose it in small leaks"
date: 2027-07-11
pageDescription: "Peace rarely goes in one big blow. It leaks out through tiny reactions you barely notice."
---

We think peace is lost in one big blow. Usually it goes the way air leaves a tyre, slowly, through small holes you barely notice.

A short reply that stung. A comment you replayed on the drive home. A scroll that left you tense. None of them felt like much on their own. But by the end of the day your calm is flat, and you cannot quite say where it went.

That is worth knowing, because it changes where you look. You do not just guard against the one huge provocation. You watch the small leaks, the tiny reactions that drain you a little at a time.

Catch a few of those each day and something shifts. Not because you handled a crisis, but because you stopped losing peace in places you never noticed it was going.
