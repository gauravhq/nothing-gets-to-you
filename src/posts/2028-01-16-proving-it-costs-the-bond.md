---
title: "Proving it costs the bond"
date: 2028-01-16
pageDescription: "You can win the point and lose the person. Ask which one you would rather keep."
---

You can be completely right and still damage the thing you care about. Winning the point can cost you the bond.

You press until the other person feels small, or cornered, or foolish. You get your win. And something between you cools. They trust you a little less, open up a little less, brace a little more next time. The record now says you were right. The relationship says you were hard to be close to.

This is worth weighing in the moment, because the trade is easy to miss. Being right feels free. It is not. Sometimes it costs the very connection the argument was inside of.

So before you drive the point home, ask which you would rather keep, the point or the person. Now and then the point truly matters. Far more often, the bond is worth more than being proven right, and the kinder path keeps both.
