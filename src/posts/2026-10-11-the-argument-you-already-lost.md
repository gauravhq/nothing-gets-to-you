---
title: "The argument you already lost"
date: 2026-10-11
pageDescription: "The argument ended hours ago, and you are still winning it in your head."
---

There is a particular kind of argument that does the most damage, and it is one you have alone.

Someone said the thing hours ago. Everyone else has moved on. And you are still going, rehearsing, building the perfect case, delivering the line you wish you had said. In that courtroom you are the lawyer, the judge, and the jury, and you win every single time, because the other person is not there.

And it costs you the evening.

The work is to notice when you have started, and to step out. Not by pretending it did not happen, but by seeing that replaying it changes nothing and steals something. The argument is over. You are the only one still in the room. You are allowed to leave.
