---
title: "The third option nobody teaches you"
date: 2022-10-09
pageDescription: "Most of us learned two ways to handle a sting: swallow it or fire back. There is a third."
---

When something gets under your skin, most of us reach for one of two moves. We swallow it, and carry it around for the rest of the day. Or we fire back, and spend the next few hours living with what we said.

Both feel like the only options. Neither leaves us any calmer.

There is a third one, and almost no one is taught it. It is the small space between what happens and what you do next. In that space, you get to choose your response instead of having it chosen for you before you are even awake to it.

Not reacting is not going numb, and it is not staying silent when something is wrong. It is noticing the heat rise and deciding, on purpose, what to do with it.

That pause is a skill. It is clumsy at first, and it gets steadier with practice. This week, the only thing to try is to notice the moment the heat arrives, before you move. You do not have to do anything with it yet. Just see it. That noticing is where all of it begins.
