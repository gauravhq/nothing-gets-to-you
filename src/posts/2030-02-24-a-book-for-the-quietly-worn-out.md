This is a book for the quietly worn out. Not the people in crisis, but the ones who are mostly fine and mostly tired, worn down by a hundred small reactions a day.

The cold email that ruined an afternoon. The argument replayed all night. The scroll that left you tense. The snapped reply you regretted. None of it is a catastrophe. All of it adds up, until you feel frayed in a way you cannot quite name.

If that is you, you are the person this was written for. Not because something is wrong with you, but because nobody ever taught you how to stop leaking peace through all those small holes.

That is what this offers. Not a dramatic overhaul, but a quieter, steadier way of moving through ordinary days. A way to stop being worn thin by the small stuff, so that the ordinary hours feel lighter, and you end more of them with something left in the tank.
