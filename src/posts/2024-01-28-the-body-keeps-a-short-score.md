---
title: "The body keeps a short score"
date: 2024-01-28
pageDescription: "Your body logs a reaction before your mind admits it. That tightness is a signal you can read."
---

Reactions are not just in the mind. The body keeps its own record, and it is quick to show.

The tight chest. The clenched jaw. The stomach in a knot. The heart still going long after the moment passed. You may tell yourself you are fine, but the body has already logged it, and it does not lie as easily as the mind does.

This is worth noticing, because the body often knows before you do. That tightness is a signal. Something got to me. Reading it early gives you a chance to pause before the reaction fully takes hold.

And it works the other way too. When you let a provocation pass, the body relaxes, and you feel the difference as real ease, not just a nice idea. Your calm is not just a mood. It is something your body carries, or does not, all day.
