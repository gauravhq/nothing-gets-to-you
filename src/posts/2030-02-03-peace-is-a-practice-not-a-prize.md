---
title: "Peace is a practice, not a prize"
date: 2030-02-03
pageDescription: "There is no finish line where you arrive unshakable and done. Peace is a garden you keep tending, for good."
---

It helps to know what you are actually aiming at. Peace is not a prize you win once and keep. It is a practice you return to, for good.

We imagine there is a finish line, a day when we will have finally arrived, calm and unshakable, done. There is no such day. Even the steadiest people still get provoked, still slip, still have to come back to their center. The difference is that they have made returning a practice, something they do again and again, not a state they reached and locked in.

This is freeing, not discouraging. It means you do not have to be perfect, ever. You just have to keep practicing. There is no failing a practice, only continuing it.

So let go of the idea that one day you will be finished. You will not, and that is fine. Peace is not a trophy on a shelf. It is a garden you keep tending, a thing you keep coming back to. Aim not to arrive, but to keep practicing, and you will find peace along the way.
