---
title: "The weather and the sky"
date: 2027-07-04
pageDescription: "You are the sky. Your reactions are weather passing through it."
---

Here is a picture worth keeping. You are the sky. Your reactions are the weather.

Storms roll through. Anger, worry, a flash of hurt. They can be loud, and for a while they fill the whole view. But they are not the sky. They are passing through it.

The mistake we make is thinking we are the storm. We say I am furious, I am a wreck, as if the weather were the whole of us. It is not. It is moving across something much wider and steadier that was there before the cloud and will be there after.

When a hard feeling comes, try watching it the way you would watch weather. This will pass through. You do not have to become it. The sky never has to fight the storm. It just holds it, and waits, and clears.
