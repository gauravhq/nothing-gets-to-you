---
title: "The cost of chasing calm"
date: 2023-12-10
pageDescription: "Straining to be calm becomes its own agitation. You do not force peace, you allow it."
---

Here is a strange trap. Trying hard to be calm can become its own kind of agitation.

You grip. You tell yourself to relax, now, and get frustrated when it does not come. You treat peace like a target to hit, and the trying tightens the very thing you wanted to loosen.

Calm is not something you force. It is more like something you allow. You stop feeding the fire, you let the spike fall, you give it a minute, and steadiness returns on its own. Pushing for it harder just adds more heat.

So when you notice yourself straining to be calm, ease off the strain itself. You do not have to win calm. You have to stop fighting so hard. Put the effort into not reacting, and let the peace arrive by itself, the way quiet returns to a room once you stop shouting in it.
