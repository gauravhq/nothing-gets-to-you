---
title: "Letting someone else shine"
date: 2028-06-25
pageDescription: "The urge to top someone else's moment is the ego twitching. Letting them have it takes quiet strength."
---

There is a small, generous move that takes real strength. Letting someone else shine.

Someone tells a story, shares a win, has the good idea. The ego twitches to match it, to top it, to bring it back to you. What is my bigger story, my better idea? Letting that pass, and simply being glad for them, is harder than it sounds.

But it is one of the surest signs of a settled person. They do not need to be the brightest thing in every room. They can hold the light on someone else and feel no smaller for it.

And people love being around that. Nothing warms a person like being genuinely celebrated instead of quietly competed with.

So next time the urge to top someone rises, let it go, and let them have the moment. You lose nothing. You give something rare. And the quiet strength it takes is felt by everyone in the room.
