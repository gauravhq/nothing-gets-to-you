---
title: "Your triggers are a map"
date: 2024-06-30
pageDescription: "Your triggers are not just problems. They are a map of what in you still needs care."
---

It is easy to see triggers as flaws, the embarrassing places where you lose your cool. They are also something more useful. They are a map.

Each one marks a spot that still needs care. The thing that reliably sets you off is showing you where an old hurt lives, where a fear still sits, where you have not fully healed. That is useful information, not just a weakness to hide.

Most people run from their triggers, or feel ashamed of them. But if you turn and study yours, they will tell you exactly where the work is. The criticism that stings, the being ignored that burns, the disrespect that enrages, each points somewhere real.

So treat your triggers as a map rather than a verdict. They are not proof that something is wrong with you. They are directions to the places in you that are asking, quietly, to be tended.
