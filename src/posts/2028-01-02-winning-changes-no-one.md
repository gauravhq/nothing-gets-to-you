---
title: "Winning changes no one"
date: 2028-01-02
pageDescription: "People almost never change their mind because they lost. Winning rarely gets you the thing you wanted."
---

We argue as if winning will change the other person. It almost never does.

Think about the last time you were argued into a corner. Did you walk away believing the other side? Or did you dig in, feel resentful, and hold your view even harder in private? That is what usually happens. People do not change their mind because they lost. They change it slowly, on their own, when they feel safe enough to.

So winning the argument rarely gets you the thing you wanted, which was for them to see it your way. You get the point and lose the goal.

If you actually want to move someone, pressure is the wrong tool. A calm question, a bit of respect, room to save face, those do far more than a victory. And if you do not need to move them at all, then there was even less reason to fight.
