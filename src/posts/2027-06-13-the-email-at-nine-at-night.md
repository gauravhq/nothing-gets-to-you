---
title: "The email at nine at night"
date: 2027-06-13
pageDescription: "The message that arrives late and quietly ruins your sleep. You can choose not to let it."
---

There is a particular message that does outsized damage: the one that arrives at nine at night and quietly ruins your sleep.

It lands after the day should be done. Your guard is down, your reserves are low, and the reactive mind treats it as urgent, as something that must be answered now, tonight, before you can rest.

Almost none of them are. The email at nine at night can be the email at nine in the morning, answered by a rested version of you who will handle it better and mind it less.

So make the rule now. After a certain hour, work waits. You do not open it, you do not answer it, you do not let it into the part of the night that is supposed to restore you. Protecting your peace sometimes means protecting your sleep from the things that would happily take it.
