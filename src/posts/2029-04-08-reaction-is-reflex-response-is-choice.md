---
title: "Reaction is reflex, response is choice"
date: 2029-04-08
pageDescription: "A reaction happens to you. A response has a gap of choice in it. The aim is to turn more of one into the other."
---

The whole of this comes down to one distinction. A reaction is a reflex. A response is a choice.

A reaction happens to you. The trigger lands and the behavior fires, fast, automatic, before you have decided anything. You did not choose it. It chose you. A response is different. There is a gap in it, a moment where you saw the trigger, considered, and picked what to do.

Same situation, two completely different ways of meeting it. One is you on autopilot. The other is you at the wheel.

The goal is not to feel less or care less. It is to turn more of your reactions into responses, to put a little choice where there used to be only reflex. Every time you do, you take back a piece of your life from the autopilot. Reaction, response. The difference between them is where all the freedom is, and it is one you can practice.
