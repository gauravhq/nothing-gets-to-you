---
title: "Calm is a skill, not a personality"
date: 2022-12-25
pageDescription: "You are not either born steady or not. It is built, slowly, in ordinary days."
---

It is easy to believe some people are just calm, and you are not one of them. That steadiness is a personality you either got or you did not.

It is not. Calm is a skill, and skills are built.

You will practice, and forget, and snap, and catch yourself a little sooner the next time. It is built the way anything solid is built, slowly, in ordinary days, one small choice not to react.

A few quiet practices do more than any grand resolution. A little stillness, some minutes with no screen and no one to answer. A little looking back, revisiting a moment you reacted and asking, gently, what happened there. And less of whatever reliably makes you reactive, the feeds and the fights that leave you agitated.

You will have good days and bad days. And slowly, over months, a few more good ones, until the bad days become the exception. That is the real change, not a set of tricks you perform, but a gradual shift in how quickly you notice the heat and step back from it.
