date: 2029-07-08

---

Every time you feel provoked by something online, remember. It was designed to provoke you.

The feed is not showing you a fair picture of the world. It is showing you whatever keeps you engaged, and what keeps you engaged, above all, is what makes you react. Outrage, fear, envy, the urge to argue. The most reaction-provoking content rises to the top, on purpose, because reactions are what the system is built to gather.

So the anger you feel scrolling is not quite yours. It was engineered. A machine studied millions of people and learned exactly what buttons to push, and then it pushed yours.

This does not mean you are weak for reacting. It means you are up against something designed to make you react. And knowing that is a kind of armor. When the feed hands you something enraging, you can pause and see the machinery. It wants a reaction. You do not have to give it one just because it asked so cleverly.
