---
title: "Let them be wrong"
date: 2024-05-26
pageDescription: "You are not required to correct every mistaken view. Letting someone be wrong is often the calmer road."
---

You do not have to correct every wrong thing you hear. You are allowed to let people be wrong.

This sounds strange, because there is a strong pull to set the record straight. Someone says something mistaken and your whole body wants to fix it. But most of those corrections change nothing, and they cost you calm and often cost the mood of the room.

Letting someone be wrong is not weakness or dishonesty. It is choosing not to spend your energy on a thing that does not need your fixing. Their wrong opinion about a film, a fact, a plan, will survive without your correction, and so will you.

Save your voice for the wrong things that actually cause harm. For the rest, you can let it sit there, unfixed, and feel how light it is to not be the one who has to correct everyone.
