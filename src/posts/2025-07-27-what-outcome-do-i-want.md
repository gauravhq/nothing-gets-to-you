There is one question that turns a reaction into a response. What outcome do I actually want here?

A reaction has no goal. It just discharges. You fire back because it feels good in the moment, with no thought for where it leads. A response starts from the end. Before you act, you ask what you want to come of this, and then you choose the move that gets you there.

Often the honest answer surprises you. You wanted to be closer to this person, and yet you were about to say something that would push them away. You wanted the problem solved, and you were about to start a fight that would bury it.

So in the hot moment, ask it. What do I want the outcome to be? Then act toward that, not away from it. The reaction serves the heat. The response serves the goal. And keeping your eye on the goal is what keeps you from wrecking the very thing you wanted.
