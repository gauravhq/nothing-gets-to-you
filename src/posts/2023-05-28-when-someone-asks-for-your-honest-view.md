---
title: "When someone asks for your honest view"
date: 2023-05-28
pageDescription: "There is a moment when speaking is exactly right. Do not miss it by playing calm."
---

Not reacting can tip into never saying anything real, and that is its own kind of loss.

There is a clear moment when speaking is exactly right: when someone genuinely asks for your honest view and actually wants it. Staying safely quiet then is not calm. It is withholding.

The skill is not silence. It is telling the loud, hot, pointless urge to react apart from the quiet, real thing that is worth saying. When a person you trust asks what you think, and it matters, that is a green light, not a test of your composure.

Say it plainly, and kindly, and once. You can be steady and honest at the same time. In fact, steadiness is what lets you say the true thing well, without the heat that would have made them stop listening.
