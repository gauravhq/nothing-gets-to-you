---
title: "Being understood over being right"
date: 2027-12-26
pageDescription: "Under the need to be right is often a simpler wish, to be understood. Name that and the fight softens."
---

Under a lot of arguments is a want that has nothing to do with facts. You do not really need to win. You need to feel understood.

Watch closely next time. The heat often rises not because the other person is wrong, but because you feel unseen. Unheard. Like your side does not count. So you push harder on being right, when the real ache is being understood.

This is worth knowing, because it points to a better move. Instead of proving your case louder, you can say the real thing. I feel like you are not hearing me. That gets closer to what you actually want than any won point.

And it works the other way. When you make the other person feel understood, their need to fight drops too. Most arguments are two people both trying to be understood, mistaking it for a contest to be right.
