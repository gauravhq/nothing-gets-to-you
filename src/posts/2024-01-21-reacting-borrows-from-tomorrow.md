---
title: "Reacting borrows from tomorrow"
date: 2024-01-21
pageDescription: "A blowup tonight reaches forward and takes energy from the morning you have not lived yet."
---

A reaction tonight does not stay in tonight. It reaches forward and takes from tomorrow.

You blow up in the evening, and the next morning you wake up tired, a little ashamed, carrying the residue. The fight is over, but the fog is still there. Now you face a new day with less than you had, because part of you is still cleaning up the old one.

This is the hidden cost we rarely count. A reaction is not paid for in the moment. It is paid for in the hours and days after, in the energy you do not have for the things that matter.

So when the heat rises late at night, remember who picks up the bill. Tomorrow you does. Give that person a break. Let the thing wait. A rested, clear morning is worth far more than the release of reacting while the day is already ending.
