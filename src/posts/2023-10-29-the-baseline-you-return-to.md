---
title: "The baseline you return to"
date: 2023-10-29
pageDescription: "Steadiness is not never wobbling. It is how fast you find your way back."
---

Peace is not a state you hold without a break. It is a baseline you keep coming back to.

Nobody stays calm every hour. You will get knocked off. You will react, worry, spin for a while. The question is not whether you leave your center. It is how quickly you find your way back.

Think of it like balance. A steady person is not one who never wobbles. It is one who has a strong sense of true north and returns to it fast, so the wobble is short and small.

So do not measure yourself by whether you ever lose your calm. Measure the return. Each time you notice you have drifted and come back, that path gets easier to walk. Over time the baseline itself rises, and the trips away from it get shorter.
