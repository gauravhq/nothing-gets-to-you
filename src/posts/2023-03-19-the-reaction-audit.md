Now and then, revisit a moment you reacted, and ask, gently, what happened there.

Not to punish yourself. To learn. What was the trigger? What did it cost? What would you do next time?

This one small habit does more than any resolution to make you a calmer person. Most of us react and move on, and react again the same way next week, because we never look. Looking, even briefly, is how the same button starts to work a little less well.

You do not need a journal, though you can use one. You just need to be willing, once in a while, to turn around and study a moment that got you, instead of hurrying past it. Awareness is not the whole skill, but it is the first step, and without it the rest never starts.
