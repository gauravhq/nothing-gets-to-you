---
title: "The weekly look back"
date: 2030-01-13
pageDescription: "A few quiet minutes each week to review where you lost it and what set you off is how patterns finally shift."
---

A small habit does more for your steadiness than any grand resolution. A weekly look back.

Once a week, take a few quiet minutes to review. Where did I lose it this week? What set me off? What did I handle well? What would I do differently? You are not judging yourself. You are studying the week, gently, to learn.

Most people never do this. They move through the weeks reacting the same ways, never pausing to see the patterns. And so the patterns never change. The weekly look back is how you catch them, name them, and slowly shift them.

It does not take long. A few minutes on a Sunday, a short note in a journal, a quiet moment of review. Over time, this small habit reveals your triggers, tracks your progress, and keeps you honest about how you are actually doing. Steadiness is built through awareness, and the weekly look back is one of the simplest ways to keep that awareness alive, week after week.
