---
title: "The size tells you something"
date: 2024-06-23
pageDescription: "When a reaction is far bigger than the event, the extra size points at an old wound, not the moment."
---

When your reaction is way out of proportion to what happened, the extra size is telling you something.

A small comment, and you are furious for an hour. A minor slight, and it stings for days. If the moment was small but the reaction was huge, the gap is the clue. Most of that heat is not about this. It is about something older that this moment brushed against.

The outsized part is a signpost. It points back to a wound that is still tender, a fear that got poked, a pattern from long before the person in front of you showed up.

So instead of only asking what they did, ask why it landed so hard. The answer usually has little to do with them. And once you see the old thing underneath, the present moment shrinks back to its real, much smaller size.
