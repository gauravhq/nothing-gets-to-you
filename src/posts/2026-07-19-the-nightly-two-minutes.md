title: "The nightly two minutes"
date: 2026-07-19
pageDescription: "Two minutes of gentle review before sleep slowly makes your patterns visible, and visible patterns are the ones that change."
---

A tiny nightly habit does more for your steadiness than any big resolution. Two minutes, at the day's end, to look back.

Before sleep, take a moment. Where did I react today? What set me off? What did I handle well? What would I do differently? That is it. Two minutes of gentle review, not judgment, just noticing.

This works because steadiness is built through awareness, and awareness needs a moment to form. Rush from day to day with no reflection, and you keep reacting the same ways, blind to the patterns. Two minutes of looking back each night slowly makes the patterns visible, and visible patterns are the ones that change.

So build it in. Two minutes before sleep. You do not need a journal, though it helps. You just need the small, regular habit of turning around and studying the day. It is short enough to actually keep, and over weeks and months, it quietly rewires how you meet the world.
