---
title: "Silence is not agreement"
date: 2029-01-21
pageDescription: "You can hear something you disagree with and simply not respond. Your quiet is not a signature."
---

One fear keeps us correcting. If I stay quiet, they will think I agree. So we jump in to mark our disagreement, even when it changes nothing.

But silence is not agreement. You are allowed to hear something you disagree with and simply not respond, without it meaning you endorse it. Your quiet is not a signature. It is just you choosing not to spend energy on this one.

This frees you from a lot of pointless corrections. You do not have to announce your disagreement with every wrong thing to keep your integrity. You know what you think. You do not need to prove it to everyone, every time, out loud.

So when you let something pass, let go of the worry that your silence says yes. It does not. It says this is not worth my breath right now, which is often the truest and calmest thing you could say. You can disagree deeply and still say nothing at all.
