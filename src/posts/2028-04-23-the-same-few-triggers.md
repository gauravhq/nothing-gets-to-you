---
title: "The same few triggers"
date: 2028-04-23
pageDescription: "Most of us are not set off by a hundred things. We have a short, repeating list. Learn yours."
---

Here is something oddly reassuring. You do not have a hundred triggers. Most people have a short list, the same few things, over and over.

Being disrespected. Being ignored. Being told what to do. Being criticized. Feeling controlled. For any one person, it usually comes down to a handful, and those same few explain most of the reactions across a whole life.

This is good news, because a short list is learnable. You are not fighting endless enemies. You are getting to know maybe three or four recurring ones. And once you can name yours, you start to see them coming from a distance.

So take a quiet look back. What actually sets you off, again and again? Write the short list down. There is real power in knowing that your reactions, which feel so varied and unpredictable, mostly run on the same few buttons you can finally learn by name.
