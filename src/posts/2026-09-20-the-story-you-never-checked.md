---
title: "The story you never checked"
date: 2026-09-20
pageDescription: "Between what happens and how you feel, there is a story you rarely stop to check."
---

Someone is short with you in a message, and instantly your mind hands you a reason. They are rude. They do not respect me. They are trying to undermine me.

You did not choose that story. It arrived on its own, in a heartbeat, and it is often one of the less generous explanations available. And then you react, not to what happened, but to the story you never once stopped to check.

Usually the story is incomplete. The short message was written by someone between two meetings, or worried about a sick parent, or just tired, and it had nothing to do with you.

The practice is small. When something stings, notice the story your mind supplied, and hold it a little more loosely. You do not have to believe the first thing you tell yourself.
