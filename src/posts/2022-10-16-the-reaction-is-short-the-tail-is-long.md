---
title: "The reaction is short. The tail is long."
date: 2022-10-16
pageDescription: "The flare-up lasts a few seconds. The cost of it can follow you for days."
---

A reaction is quick. A few sharp seconds, and it is over.

The tail is not. The argument that changed nothing still follows you onto the drive home. The reply you fired off in ten seconds gets reread all week. The remark at dinner is still sitting in your chest at midnight.

That is the part we forget when the heat is on us. In the moment, the reaction feels free, like it costs nothing to let it out. The bill just arrives later, quietly, in a mood that will not lift and a night you do not sleep well.

Seeing this clearly is its own small protection. The next time you feel the pull to snap back, you are not really choosing between saying it and not saying it. You are choosing between a few seconds of relief and a long tail you will carry alone.

Most of the time, once you see the tail, the urge to react loses a surprising amount of its grip.
