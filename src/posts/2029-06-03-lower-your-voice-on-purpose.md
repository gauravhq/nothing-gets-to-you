---
title: "Lower your voice on purpose"
date: 2029-06-03
pageDescription: "A quiet, steady voice in a heated moment carries more authority than a loud one, and pulls the other voice down."
---

Here is a small, almost magical move. In a tense moment, lower your voice on purpose.

Everything pulls the other way. As heat rises, voices rise with it, and it feels natural to get louder to be heard. But going quieter does something a raised voice never can. It draws people in. It signals control. And it makes the other person, almost without noticing, lower theirs to match.

A quiet, steady voice in a heated moment carries more authority than a loud one. Loud reads as out of control. Quiet reads as in command. And it breaks the escalation, because two people cannot easily keep shouting when one of them keeps getting softer.

So the next time things heat up, try it deliberately. Drop your volume. Slow your words. Speak just a little more softly than feels natural. You will feel the temperature of the whole exchange come down, and you will have done it without saying anything about calming down at all.
