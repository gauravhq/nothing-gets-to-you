---
title: "What if they are a little right"
date: 2028-01-23
pageDescription: "Loosening your grip on being totally right lets the heat out and gets you closer to what is real."
---

Here is a small move that takes a lot of heat out of a disagreement. Ask yourself, quietly, what if they are a little bit right?

Most arguments treat it as all or nothing. I am right, you are wrong. But reality is rarely that clean. Usually there is a grain of truth on the other side, a point you had not weighed, a way you overstated your case.

When you can admit that, even to yourself, the grip loosens. You stop defending a total position you cannot actually hold, and you get closer to what is real, which is usually somewhere in the middle.

It also disarms the other person. Nothing softens a fight faster than one side saying, you have a point there. Try it. You do not lose anything by granting them a piece of the truth. You often gain the whole conversation.
