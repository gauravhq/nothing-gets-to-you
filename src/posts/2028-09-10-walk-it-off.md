---
title: "Walk it off"
date: 2028-09-10
pageDescription: "A reaction lives in the body too. Movement settles it faster than sitting still and stewing ever will."
---

Sometimes the best pause is not mental at all. It is physical. You get up and walk it off.

When a reaction has you wound tight, sitting in it can make it worse, the thoughts spinning, the body clenched. Moving changes the state. A short walk, a lap around the block, even just standing up and stepping outside, shifts something. The heat has somewhere to go besides your next sentence.

This works because a reaction is in the body as much as the mind, and the body responds to movement faster than to reasoning. You cannot always think your way calm. You can often walk your way there.

So when you feel yourself boiling, and you can, get up and move. Do not make the big decision or send the message while you are still seated in the heat. Walk first. Ten minutes of moving does more to settle you than an hour of stewing, and you come back with a clearer head and a cooler chest.
