---
title: "Right and happy, pick one"
date: 2024-03-24
pageDescription: "In a heated moment you often get to be right or to be at peace. Choosing peace is not losing."
---

In a lot of heated moments, you get a quiet choice. You can be right, or you can be at peace. In that minute, you often cannot have both.

Being right means pressing the point, proving it, making sure they see it. Being at peace means letting the moment settle, even with the record uncorrected. The first feels urgent. The second feels like losing.

It is not losing. It is choosing what you actually want more. And most of the time, a day or a week later, you will not remember the point you were so sure about. You will remember whether the evening was calm or wrecked.

So when you feel the pull to be right, ask the honest question. Do I want to be right here, or do I want to be at peace? You are allowed to pick peace. Often it is the wiser buy.
