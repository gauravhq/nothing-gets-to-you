---
title: "Hungry, angry, tired"
date: 2024-07-14
pageDescription: "Some of your worst reactions are not about the moment. They are hunger and tiredness in disguise."
---

Before you decide a reaction was about the person in front of you, run a quick check. Are you hungry? Are you tired? Have you been running on empty?

So many of our worst reactions are not really about the trigger. They are about the state we were already in. On a full stomach and a good night's sleep, the comment rolls off. Hungry and exhausted, the same comment feels like the last straw.

This is worth building into a habit, because it is easy to miss in the heat. You feel sure it is about them. Often it is about your fuel gauge sitting near empty.

So when you feel yourself getting sharp, ask what state you are in first. If the honest answer is tired, hungry, stretched thin, then be gentle, feed yourself, rest, and hold off on any big reaction. Half the time the trigger dissolves once the tank is filled.
