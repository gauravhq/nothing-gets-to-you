---
title: "The thermostat, not the thermometer"
date: 2023-12-24
pageDescription: "A thermometer takes the room's temperature. A thermostat sets it. You can choose which you are."
---

There are two ways to be in a room. Like a thermometer, or like a thermostat.

A thermometer just reads the temperature. If the room is hot, it goes hot. If the room is tense, it goes tense. It has no setting of its own. It only reflects what is around it.

A thermostat has a set point. It holds a temperature and, if anything, brings the room toward it. It is not pushed around by the weather in the room. It steadies the room instead.

Most of us live as thermometers. Someone else is angry, so we get angry. The mood is anxious, so we catch it. But you can choose to be the thermostat. Hold your own steadiness, and let it lower the heat around you.

You do not have to match the room. You can set it.
