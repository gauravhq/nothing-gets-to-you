title: "The door a reaction closes"
date: 2024-02-25
pageDescription: "Reacting shuts a door a calm response would have kept open, to a repair, a fix, a person who still trusts you."
---

Every reaction closes a door that a calm response would have kept open.

You fire back at the colleague, and the chance to work well together narrows. You snap at your teenager, and they decide not to tell you the next thing. You lose it with the person on the phone, and the help you needed dries up. In the moment the reaction felt justified. It also quietly cost you the better outcome.

This is the opportunity we rarely count. Reacting does not just cost energy and sleep. It costs the door, the one that leads to a repaired relationship, a solved problem, a person who still trusts you.

So when you feel the pull to react, remember there is usually a door behind it. Stay steady, and it stays open. You can almost always come back to a calm response later. The slammed door is much harder to reopen.
