How you start the morning quietly sets the tone for the whole day. It is worth protecting.

Grab the phone the moment you wake, and you begin the day already reacting, already flooded with other people's urgencies before your feet hit the floor. Start slow, with a few quiet minutes that are yours, and you enter the day steady, from a fuller place, harder to knock off balance.

This is not about a perfect elaborate routine. It is about the first few minutes. A little quiet before the noise. A breath before the feed. A moment that belongs to you before it belongs to everyone else.

So guard the start. Give yourself a small buffer between waking and the world rushing in. It does not have to be long. Even a few unhurried minutes change the tone you carry into everything after. The morning is the one part of the day you can most easily protect, and protecting it makes the rest of the day easier to meet.
