---
title: "Personal rules for a peaceful life"
date: 2027-05-09
pageDescription: "A few rules, decided once, save you from deciding again in every hot moment."
---

A few personal rules, decided once and kept, do more for your peace than endless willpower in the moment.

They are yours to write. A few that tend to help: I do not send messages written in anger. I make my case once, then let it go. I do not argue with strangers online. I answer stings in an hour, not a second. When I am depleted, I assume my reactions are turned up too high, and act accordingly.

The value of a rule is that it takes the decision out of the hot moment, where you are least able to make it well. You are not relying on being wise under pressure. You are following a line your calm self already drew.

Write two or three of your own. Keep them simple enough to remember. Then let them quietly protect you, again and again, from the version of you that reacts first and thinks later.
