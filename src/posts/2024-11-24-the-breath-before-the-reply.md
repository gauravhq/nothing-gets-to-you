title: "The breath before the reply"
date: 2024-11-24

---

The whole practice can fit inside a single breath. One slow breath before you reply.

That is often all it takes. Something lands, the heat rises, and instead of firing, you take one breath. In and out. In that small space, the hottest version of your response comes and goes, and a steadier one has room to form.

It sounds too simple to matter. It works because the reaction lives in the first second, and a breath gives that second somewhere to go besides your mouth. You are not suppressing anything. You are just letting the spike pass its peak before you act.

You do not need a whole method. You need one breath, taken on purpose, at the exact moment you most want to skip it. Practice it on small things, the mild annoyances, so it is there for the big ones. One breath is small enough to actually do, and large enough to change what happens next.
