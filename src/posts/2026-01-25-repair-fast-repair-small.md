---
title: "Repair fast, repair small"
date: 2026-01-25
pageDescription: "A quick, genuine word soon after mends more than a grand apology days later, once resentment has set."
---

When you have hurt someone with a reaction, repair fast and repair small.

Fast, because the longer you wait, the more the moment hardens. A quick, honest word soon after, while it is still fresh, mends things far more easily than a big apology days later, once resentment has had time to set.

Small, because the repair does not need to be grand. It needs to be genuine. A simple, I overreacted, I am sorry, offered soon, does more than an elaborate gesture offered late. You do not have to make a production of it. You have to be honest, and you have to be timely.

So when you catch yourself having reacted badly, do not let it sit and fester while you work up the courage for some perfect apology. Go now, keep it simple, and say the true thing. Fast and small beats late and large. The point is not to perform remorse. It is to mend the thread quickly, before the knot gets hard to undo.
