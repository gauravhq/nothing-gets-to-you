---
title: "Being right is cheap"
date: 2023-02-05
pageDescription: "Almost anyone can be right about something. Staying steady while you disagree is rare."
---

Being right is cheap. Almost anyone can be right about something, and being right rarely changes how the moment feels.

Staying steady while you disagree with someone is the rare thing. It is harder, and it is worth far more than a hundred won arguments.

We treat being right like a prize. We collect it, defend it, replay it. But you can be completely right and still spend the night unable to sleep, still lose the relationship, still hand your calm to someone who does not even know they took it.

So the next time you feel the pull to prove a point, ask what you are actually after. If it is the truth, say it once and let it stand. If it is the feeling of winning, notice that the feeling is cheap, and it costs more than it is worth.
