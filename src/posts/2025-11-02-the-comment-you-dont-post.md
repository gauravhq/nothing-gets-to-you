There is an online cousin of the email you do not send. It is the comment you do not post.

Something online provokes you, and your fingers are already typing the reply, the correction, the sharp remark, the devastating point. The comment box is right there, one tap from the world. So easy. So instant. So often regretted.

The move is the same as any pause. Type it if you must, then do not post it. Let the urge pass. Put the phone down. Come back later, if at all. Almost every time, the comment that felt so necessary will look pointless in an hour, a bit of heat you were about to hand to strangers who did not deserve your peace.

The unposted comment costs you nothing and saves you plenty. No pile-on, no argument with people you will never meet, no hours lost to a thread. Write it for yourself if it helps. Then close the app, and keep the afternoon the comment would have taken.
