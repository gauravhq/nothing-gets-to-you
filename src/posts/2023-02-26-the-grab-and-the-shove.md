---
title: "The grab and the shove"
date: 2023-02-26
pageDescription: "Two moves the ego makes in a heated conversation. Naming them takes their power."
---

In a heated conversation, the ego tends to make one of two moves. The grab: reaching for the last word, the winning point, the little nod that says yes, you were right. The shove: pushing away anything that feels like being wrong, because in that moment being wrong can feel like danger.

Both are fast, both are automatic, and both pull you straight out of calm.

The practice is to name the move while it is happening. That is a grab. That is a shove. You do not have to obey it, and you do not have to shame it either. You notice it, name it, and then choose.

Most of the time, once it has been named, the urgency drains out, and you find you did not need to say the thing at all.
