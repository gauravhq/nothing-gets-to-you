---
title: "We are lawyers, not judges"
date: 2026-10-18
pageDescription: "Make your case once, plainly, and then let it go. That is the whole move."
---

When we disagree, most of us behave like lawyers, not judges. We are not weighing the truth. We are building a case for the side we already picked, and the goal is to win.

A judge does something different. A judge makes the case for what is true, once, and then lets the decision go where it goes.

Here is the move that keeps your peace. Offer your honest view, clearly, one time. Then let it land where it lands. You are not responsible for making the other person agree, and trying to is where the heat and the hours go.

Say it well. Say it once. Then step back. Most of the time you will find that letting go of the outcome is what actually lets you keep your calm.
