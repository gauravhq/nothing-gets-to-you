---
title: "Awareness is the first step"
date: 2023-06-18
pageDescription: "You cannot change a pattern you cannot see. Noticing is where all of it begins."
---

You cannot loosen a grip you cannot feel. You cannot change a pattern you cannot see.

So before any technique, before any rule, the work is simply to notice. To catch the heat as it rises, a half-second sooner each time. To see the story your mind supplied. To feel the pull to react before you obey it.

This sounds too simple to matter, but it is most of the skill. The reaction that owns you is the one that happens before you are awake to it. The moment you can see it happening, even a little, it loses a startling amount of its power.

You do not have to fix anything today. For a day or two, just watch. Notice the moments your peace slips out the side door. Do not judge them. Just see them. That noticing is where all of it begins.
