---
title: "The person you are becoming"
date: 2027-04-25
pageDescription: "You are not trying to react perfectly. You are slowly becoming someone harder to move."
---

The goal was never to react perfectly. It is to slowly become a person who is harder to move.

You start by noticing the heat a little sooner. Then sooner than that. You catch a reaction while it is still just a flicker, before it becomes words. And one day you realize you are watching your own mind most of the time, gently, without trying, the way a calm person just seems to.

That is the real change this points at. Not a set of tricks you perform in hard moments, but a gradual shift in who you are, in how quickly you notice the reaction and step back from it.

You will not feel it happening day to day. You feel it in hindsight, when a thing that would have ruined last year's week barely touches this one. That is the person you are becoming, one small choice at a time.
