---
title: "Who actually pays"
date: 2027-09-26
pageDescription: "The person who caused your reaction is often not the one who pays for it."
---

Here is the cruel part of a reaction. The person who caused it often is not the one who pays for it.

Someone cuts you off in traffic and is gone in seconds, never knowing you exist. But you carry the anger home. And it comes out on the people who were nowhere near it. The partner who asks a simple question. The kid who wants your attention. The friend who catches the short reply.

The one who lit the match has moved on. The people you love get the fire.

That is worth sitting with, because it changes the math. Holding onto a reaction does not punish the person who wronged you. It taxes the people who did not. When you set a reaction down, you are not letting the offender win. You are protecting the ones who would have paid a bill that was never theirs.
