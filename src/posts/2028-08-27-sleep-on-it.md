---
title: "Sleep on it"
date: 2028-08-27
pageDescription: "The tired, stirred-up you at midnight should not make the big call. Let morning you decide."
---

For anything big, there is one pause that beats all the others. Sleep on it.

The angry reply, the major decision, the message that could change a relationship. Whatever it is, if it can wait until morning, let it. The version of you at eleven at night, tired and stirred up, is not the one who should be making that call. Morning you is rested, cooler, and almost always wiser.

We forget this because the night makes everything feel urgent. It must be handled now. It almost never must. Very few things are actually worse for waiting eight hours, and most are much better.

So build the rule. Nothing important gets sent or decided the night it flares up. Write it if you must, then leave it. Read it again in the morning. Nine times out of ten you will soften it, or not send it at all, and be glad the tired, hot version of you did not get the final say.
