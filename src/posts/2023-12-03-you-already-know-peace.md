title: "You already know peace"
date: 2023-12-03
pageDescription: "You are not chasing something you have never felt. You have stood there. The work is returning."
---

You are not chasing something you have never felt. You already know peace. You have been there.

Think of a quiet morning before anyone else was up. A walk where your mind finally went still. A moment after a good laugh when nothing was wrong. That settledness was not luck. It was your natural baseline showing through, once the noise dropped.

This matters because it means peace is not a far-off thing to earn. It is a place you have already stood. The work is not to invent it. It is to return to it more often, and to stop handing it away so easily.

So do not think of this as building something from nothing. Think of it as finding your way back to something you have known all along, and learning to stay there a little longer each time.
