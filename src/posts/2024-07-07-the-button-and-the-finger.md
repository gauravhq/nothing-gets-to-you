---
title: "The button and the finger"
date: 2024-07-07
pageDescription: "Others push the button, but the button is yours. That is the part you can actually work on."
---

When someone gets to you, it feels like they did it. They pushed your button. And in a way they did.

But notice the two parts. There is the finger, which is theirs, and there is the button, which is yours. They pushed. The button, though, was already there, wired to go off. Different person, different button. The same words that enrage you might not touch someone else at all.

This is not about blaming yourself. The other person may well have been out of line. It is about where your power actually is. You cannot control every finger that reaches for a button. You can, over time, work on the button itself, so it takes more to set it off.

The finger you will never fully manage. The button is yours to understand, and slowly, to defuse. That is the part worth your attention.
