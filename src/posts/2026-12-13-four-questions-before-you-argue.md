---
title: "Four questions before you argue"
date: 2026-12-13
pageDescription: "Most arguments do not survive four honest questions."
---

Before you step into an argument, four quiet questions can save you a great deal.

One: does this actually matter, or does it just feel urgent right now? Two: will speaking change anything, or do I only want to be right? Three: is this the person, and the moment, for it? Four: what will it cost me, and is that cost worth it?

Most arguments do not survive these questions. Held up to the light, the urge to engage turns out to be the heat talking, not something that needed saying.

And sometimes the answer is yes, this matters, and it is worth it. Then you speak, calmly, having already decided. Either way, the questions put a small gap between the trigger and you, which is where all the freedom lives.
