title: "The toolkit at a glance"
date: 2026-06-28
pageDescription: "When you are flooded you cannot run a whole philosophy. You need two or three simple tools within reach."
---

When you are flooded in a hot moment, you cannot run through a whole philosophy. You need a few simple tools within reach. That is what a small toolkit is for.

None of the tools is complicated. A pause before you respond. One line to steady yourself. A rule you decided in advance. A question to ask instead of a comeback. A breath. Each one is small enough to actually use when the heat is up and your thinking is not at its best.

The point of a toolkit is exactly that. It is not for calm moments when you can reason clearly. It is for the flooded ones, when you need something simple to grab.

So pick a few tools that work for you and keep them close. You do not need all of them. You need two or three you can reach for without thinking. When the moment comes, you will not have time to invent a response. You will just need one steady tool in your hand, ready.
