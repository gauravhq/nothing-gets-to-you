---
title: "Respond instead of react"
date: 2022-12-04
pageDescription: "A response has a few marks a reaction never has. Here is how to tell them apart."
---

A reaction and a response can use the same words and land completely differently.

A reaction is fast and automatic. It demands agreement, now. It is really about you, about being right, about not losing.

A response has a few marks a reaction never has. It leaves a little room. It can say let me think about that, and mean it. It separates the person from the position, so you can disagree with an idea without going to war with a human being.

The good news is you can switch mid-sentence. You will be three lines into a sharp reply, voice rising, and some quiet part of you will notice, this is going wrong. In that moment, you can stop. You can say, plainly, let me start that again, I am coming at this the wrong way. It feels awkward for one second. Then it changes the whole room.

It is a skill, so it is clumsy at first. You will manage it in easy moments and lose it in hard ones. That is normal. That is what learning anything in real time looks like.
