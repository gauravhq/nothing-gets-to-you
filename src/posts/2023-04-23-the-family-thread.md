---
title: "The family thread"
date: 2023-04-23
pageDescription: "The hardest online moments happen in the small rooms, with people you know."
---

The screen is not just strangers. Some of the hardest online moments happen in the smaller rooms: the work chat, the family thread, the group of old friends.

These are trickier, because you cannot just log off from people you love. The words sit there in writing, permanent, easy to reread and stew over at midnight. The audience is small and known, which makes the pull to respond stronger, not weaker.

The same tools still work. Buy time before you reply. Notice the story you are telling yourself about what they meant. And remember that if something really needs saying to one person, it can be said to that person, not performed for the whole group.

A reply you do not send tonight is one you can still send tomorrow, from the calm version of you, which is the only version worth sending anything from.
