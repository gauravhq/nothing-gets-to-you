---
title: "Match the pause to the stakes"
date: 2028-10-29
pageDescription: "A small annoyance needs a breath. A big provocation needs a night. Fit the length of the pause to what is at stake."
---

Not every pause needs to be the same length. A good rule is to fit the pause to the stakes.

A small annoyance needs only a breath. A sharp email deserves an hour. A big decision, or a message that could change a relationship, deserves a night, maybe several. The bigger the thing, the longer you let it sit before you act.

We get this backwards all the time. We fire off the huge, consequential reply in seconds, and then agonize for an hour over some tiny message that did not matter. The stakes and the pause are mismatched.

So when something lands, do a quick read of the stakes. How much does this actually matter? How hard would it be to undo? Then set the pause to match. Small and reversible, a breath is plenty. Big and lasting, give it real time. Matching the length of the pause to the size of the stakes keeps you from rushing the things that most deserve your patience.
