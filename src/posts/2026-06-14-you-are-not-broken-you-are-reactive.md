---
title: "You are not broken, you are reactive"
date: 2026-06-14
pageDescription: "Reactivity is not a character flaw. It is old wiring, a habit, and habits can be unlearned."
---

If you react more than you would like, hear this clearly. You are not broken. You are reactive, and reactive is not a character flaw. It is a habit, and habits can change.

We carry so much shame about our reactions. We think a calmer person is a better person, and that our short fuse says something bad about who we are. It does not. Reactivity is just old wiring, learned patterns, a nervous system doing what it learned to do. It is not a verdict on your worth.

This reframe matters, because you cannot build change on a foundation of self-hatred. If you believe you are broken, you will not do the patient work of getting steadier. You will just feel bad.

So start here instead. You are a fundamentally fine person with a reactive habit, and the habit can be unlearned. That is not a lowering of the bar. It is the honest and hopeful truth, and it is the ground everything else is built on.
