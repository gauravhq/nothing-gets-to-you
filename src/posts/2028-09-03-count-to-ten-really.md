---
title: "Count to ten, really"
date: 2028-09-03
pageDescription: "The oldest advice there is works for a plain reason: it keeps you from acting during the worst few seconds."
---

Count to ten. It is the oldest advice there is, and we roll our eyes at it. It also happens to work.

The reason is simple. The spike of a reaction is short. In those first few seconds, the heat is at its peak and your judgment is at its worst. Counting to ten does one thing, it keeps you from acting during exactly that window. By ten, the peak has usually passed, and the you who speaks is a slightly steadier one.

We dismiss it because it sounds childish. But most good advice is simple, and simple is not the same as weak. The trick is actually doing it, in the heat, when every part of you wants to skip the counting and just react.

So take the old advice seriously. When something lands, count. Slowly. All the way. It is not a magic spell. It is just a way of not handing the worst few seconds the power to speak for you.
