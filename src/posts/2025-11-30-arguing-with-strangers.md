There is a game online that cannot be won, and yet we keep playing it. Arguing with strangers.

You see a wrong opinion from someone you will never meet, and you engage. And they dig in, and you dig in, and it goes back and forth, and nobody moves an inch, and hours later you are drained and no one has changed their mind. That is not a bug in the game. That is the game. It is built to be unwinnable.

Nobody has ever been argued into agreement by a stranger in a comment thread. It does not happen. What happens is that both people leave more entrenched and more upset than before.

So the wise move is simply to not play. When you feel the pull to argue with a stranger online, remember the game has no win condition. You cannot win it. You can only lose time and peace to it. Scroll on. The stranger will keep their opinion either way, and you will keep your afternoon.
