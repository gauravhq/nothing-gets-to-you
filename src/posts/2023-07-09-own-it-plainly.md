---
title: "Own it plainly"
date: 2023-07-09
pageDescription: "A real apology is short. It does not ask the other person to comfort you."
---

When you have reacted and it landed on someone, the repair matters more than the perfect record. And the repair is simpler than we make it.

Own it plainly. Not a long, winding apology that somehow ends with the other person reassuring you. Just the plain thing. I overreacted earlier. That was not fair to you. I am sorry. Then stop.

The temptation is to over-explain, to justify, to soften it until it stops being an apology at all. But the clean version lands better. It asks for nothing. It just names what happened and takes responsibility for it.

A short, honest repair does more for a relationship than a flawless run of never slipping. People do not need you to be perfect. They need to know that when you get it wrong, you will say so, plainly, and mean it.
