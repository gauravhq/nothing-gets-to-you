---
title: "Protect your sleep"
date: 2026-03-22
pageDescription: "Few things make you more reactive than being tired. Guarding your sleep is guarding your calm."
---

Of all the things that make you reactive, few beat being tired. Which means protecting your sleep is protecting your calm.

On a bad night's sleep, your fuse is short, your patience is thin, and things that would normally roll off land hard. Rested, the same day feels manageable. The difference is not your character. It is how much sleep you got. Your steadiness sits on top of your rest.

We treat sleep as the thing to sacrifice, the hours to trade for one more episode, one more scroll, one more task. But a tired mind reacts to everything, and the cost shows up all the next day, in every short reply and lost temper.

So treat sleep as part of the practice, not separate from it. Guard your bedtime. Put the phone down earlier. Protect the hours that fill your tank. It is not indulgent. It is one of the most direct things you can do for your calm. A rested you is a steadier you, before you have made a single other choice.
