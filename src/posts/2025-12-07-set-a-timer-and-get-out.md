---
title: "Set a timer and get out"
date: 2025-12-07
pageDescription: "The feed has no natural stopping point, so decide the ending in advance. Be the one who chooses when it ends."
---

If you are going to be in the machine, go in with a plan, and the simplest plan is a time limit. Set a timer and get out.

The feed is built to be endless, with no natural stopping point. One more scroll, one more video, and suddenly an hour is gone and you feel worse than when you started. Left to itself, it does not let you go.

So you decide the ending in advance. Fifteen minutes, then out. A timer, an alarm, a hard stop. When it goes off, you close the app, regardless of the next tempting thing.

This is not about quitting entirely. It is about being the one who decides when it ends, instead of letting the endless scroll decide for you. Go in for a reason, for a set time, and then leave. Bounded time in the machine costs you little. Unbounded time is how it takes your evening and your peace. Set the timer, and honor it when it rings.
