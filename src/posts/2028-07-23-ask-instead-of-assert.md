---
title: "Ask instead of assert"
date: 2028-07-23
pageDescription: "An assertion plants an opposing flag. A question opens a door. Trade one for the other and the heat drops."
---

There is a simple swap that lowers the temperature of almost any conversation. Ask instead of assert.

When you assert, you plant a flag. This is how it is. It invites the other person to plant their opposing flag, and now you are two flags facing off. When you ask, you open a door. What makes you see it that way? Have you thought about this angle? The whole thing softens.

Assertions come from the ego wanting to be right and be seen being right. Questions come from actually wanting to understand, and people feel the difference immediately.

This does not mean you never state a view. It means you lead with curiosity more than certainty. A good question does more work than a strong claim, and it does it without the fight. Try trading one assertion for one question today, and watch how much less resistance you meet.
