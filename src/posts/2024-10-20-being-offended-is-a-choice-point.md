---
title: "Being offended is a choice point"
date: 2024-10-20
pageDescription: "Between an offensive remark and your offense there is a gap. It is small, but the choice in it is real."
---

Between something offensive and your offense, there is a gap. It is small, and it is easy to miss, but it is real.

We treat being offended as automatic. They said the thing, so of course I am offended. But look closer and there is a moment of choice in there. The same remark rolls off one person and wounds another. Part of that is the meaning you decide to give it.

This is not about excusing rude people. It is about where your power sits. You do not fully control what people say. You have more say than you think over whether you pick the offense up and carry it.

So when something lands wrong, notice the gap. I could take this as an insult, or I could let it be their bad moment and move on. You will not always choose to let it go. But knowing there is a choice point there changes everything.
