---
title: "The two arrows"
date: 2023-11-12
pageDescription: "The first arrow is what happened. The second is the one you keep firing at yourself."
---

There is an old idea worth carrying. When something hurts, two arrows can hit you.

The first arrow is the event itself. The rude remark, the bad news, the plan that fell through. You often cannot avoid that one. It lands.

The second arrow is the one you fire at yourself. The replaying, the outrage, the story about how unfair it all is, the hours you spend inside it. That arrow is not thrown by the world. You draw the bow.

Most of our pain is the second arrow. The first one stung for a moment. The second one we keep shooting, all evening, all week.

You cannot always dodge the first arrow. But you can notice your hand reaching for the second, and choose, more often, to set the bow down.
