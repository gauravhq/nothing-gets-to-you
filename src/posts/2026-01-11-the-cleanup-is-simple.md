---
title: "The cleanup is simple"
date: 2026-01-11
pageDescription: "Name it, own it, move on. A short, clean repair beats a long, tangled one that keeps the reaction alive."
---

After a reaction, the cleanup is simpler than we make it. Name it, own it, move on.

We tend to over-complicate the aftermath, either avoiding it entirely, hoping it blows over, or drowning in it, apologizing endlessly, explaining, justifying. Neither works. The clean version has three small steps. You name what happened. You take responsibility, briefly. And then you move on, without dragging it out.

I snapped at you earlier. That was not fair. I am sorry. Done. No long speech, no groveling, no making the other person comfort you. Just a clear, honest acknowledgment, and then back to normal.

This matters because a messy cleanup can do more damage than the reaction. The endless apology, the days of guilt, the walking on eggshells, all of it keeps the reaction alive far longer than it needs to be. Keep the cleanup simple. Name it, own it, move on. A short, clean repair beats a long, tangled one every time.
