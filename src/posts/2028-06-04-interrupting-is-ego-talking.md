---
title: "Interrupting is ego talking"
date: 2028-06-04
pageDescription: "Cutting in feels like enthusiasm. It quietly tells people what they were saying matters less than your point."
---

Interrupting feels like enthusiasm. A lot of the time it is the ego, unable to wait.

You cut in because your point feels too important to hold. Because you already know where they are going. Because the urge to insert yourself is stronger than the patience to let them finish. Whatever the reason, the message the other person receives is the same. What I have to say matters more than what you were saying.

This is worth watching, because interrupting quietly tells people they are not worth hearing out. Do it enough and they stop bothering to share.

The fix is small and hard. Let them finish. All the way. Feel the urge to jump in, and let it pass, the way you would let any reaction pass. The point you were bursting to make will still be there in a moment, and it will land far better on someone who felt heard first.
