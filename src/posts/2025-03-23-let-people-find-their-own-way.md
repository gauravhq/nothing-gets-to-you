---
title: "Let people find their own way"
date: 2025-03-23
pageDescription: "You cannot install understanding in another person. The lessons that stick are the ones they reach themselves."
---

There is a hard truth for anyone who likes to help. You cannot install understanding in another person. They have to find their own way to it.

You can see the mistake they are about to make. You can have the perfect advice ready. But if you force it on them, they resist, or they follow it without really getting it, and the lesson does not stick. People learn what they discover for themselves, in their own time.

This is worth accepting, because the urge to correct and steer everyone comes from a good place, wanting to spare them trouble. But constant correcting robs people of the learning that only comes from their own path.

So offer your view once if asked, then step back and let them walk it. Let people be a little wrong, take the longer road, arrive on their own. It respects them, it saves your energy, and it is usually the only way the lesson ever truly lands.
