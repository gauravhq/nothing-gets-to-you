However today went, there is one move always available. Begin again tomorrow.

Maybe you were steady all day. Maybe you lost it three times. Either way, tomorrow is a fresh start, a clean page, another chance to practice. You do not carry today's failures forward as a running debt. You set them down and begin again.

This is quietly the whole practice. Not perfection, not a flawless streak, but the willingness to keep starting over, day after day, no matter how the last one went. The reactive days do not disqualify you. They are just part of the path, and the path continues in the morning.

So when a day goes badly, do not let it become a reason to stop. Rest, forgive yourself, and begin again tomorrow. And when a day goes well, enjoy it, and begin again tomorrow all the same. Every morning is a new invitation to practice being steady. You never run out of chances to begin again.
