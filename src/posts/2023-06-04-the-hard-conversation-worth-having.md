---
title: "The hard conversation worth having"
date: 2023-06-04
pageDescription: "Some silences are not peace. They are a resentment quietly stacking into a wall."
---

Not every silence protects you. Some of them slowly poison the thing you care about.

When a real hurt goes unspoken, week after week, it does not disappear. It stacks up, brick by brick, into a wall. You go quiet to keep the peace, and one day you notice there is no peace left, just distance.

That is the sign it was the fearful kind of silence, not the chosen kind. And it means there is a conversation worth having, not in the heat of the moment, but later and calmly, in private, as a problem to solve and not a person to punish.

Staying steady is not the same as swallowing everything. Sometimes the calmest, bravest thing is to raise the hard thing, gently, before the wall gets any higher. You can be calm and still say what needs saying.
