---
title: "The outrage fades, the cost stays"
date: 2025-11-23
pageDescription: "The thing that enraged you today will be gone by Friday. The tension and hours it cost you last longer."
---

Here is the pattern with online outrage. The outrage fades fast. The cost lingers.

Something enrages you today. You feel it strongly, maybe you argue, maybe you post, maybe you just stew. And in a few days, you can barely remember what it was about. The specific outrage burned bright and then went out, replaced by the next one.

But the cost of all that outrage does not fade so quickly. The tension it put in your body. The time it took. The sourness it left. The habit it deepened, of meeting the world with a clenched jaw.

Seeing this pattern is worth it, because it reveals how bad a trade the outrage is. You give up real peace, real hours, real calm, for a feeling that will be gone by the weekend and change nothing. So next time the feed lights you up, remember. This will fade by Friday. The cost of reacting to it will stay longer than the reason ever does.
