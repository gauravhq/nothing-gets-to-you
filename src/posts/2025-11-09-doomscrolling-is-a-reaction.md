---
title: "Doomscrolling is a reaction"
date: 2025-11-09
pageDescription: "The endless scroll of dread feels passive, but it is a reaction, a reaching for the next hit of alarm."
---

We think of a reaction as a sharp, sudden thing. But there is a slow, quiet reaction that drains just as much. Doomscrolling.

You did not snap at anyone. You just kept scrolling, feeding yourself a steady drip of bad news, worst-case takes, things to worry about, long past the point where any of it helped. It feels passive, like you are just reading. But it is a reaction, a nervous reaching for the next hit of alarm, and it leaves you tense, heavy, and vaguely afraid.

Naming it as a reaction helps, because then you can meet it like any other. Notice you are doing it. Notice how it feels. And choose to stop, the way you would choose not to fire off an angry reply.

So when you catch yourself in the endless scroll of dread, name it. This is doomscrolling. This is me reacting, over and over, to things I cannot change. Then put the phone down. Your peace is worth more than staying endlessly, uselessly informed about everything that is wrong.
