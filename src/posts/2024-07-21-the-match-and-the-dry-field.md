---
title: "The match and the dry field"
date: 2024-07-21
pageDescription: "The trigger is the match. What really burns is the dry field you were already carrying."
---

A trigger is the match. But a match on wet ground does nothing. What burns is the dry field.

Someone says a careless thing. On a calm day, that match lands and goes out. But if you are already dry, already tense, already carrying a hard week, that same small match sets the whole field alight. And it feels like the match did it, when really the field was ready to burn.

This matters, because we spend so much energy blaming the match, the person, the comment, the small event. Meanwhile the thing we can actually tend is the field. How dry are you today? How much have you let build up?

You will never stop every match. Careless people are everywhere. But a field that is watered, rested, and clear does not catch so easily. Take care of the field, and most matches simply go out.
