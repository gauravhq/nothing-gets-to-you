---
title: "Why a good day can turn"
date: 2027-08-15
pageDescription: "One comment should not get to repaint eight good hours. It only does if you let it run."
---

You know the feeling. The day is going fine. Then one comment, one email, one look, and the whole thing tips.

What happened? A single thing got in and took the wheel. The other twenty good moments of the day are still true, but your mind drops them all and grips the one that stung. Now the whole day feels bad, when really one minute of it did.

This is worth seeing clearly, because it shows how little it takes, and how much power you hand over. One remark should not get to repaint eight good hours. It only does if you let it run.

Next time a good day starts to turn, name it. One thing got in. The rest of the day is still here. That small notice can stop the tip, and hand the day back to the twenty moments instead of the one.
