Sometimes the pause is as mundane as a glass of water. And that is exactly why it works.

When the heat rises, you do not need a grand technique. You need something small and physical to break the momentum. Get up. Pour a glass of water. Drink it slowly. That is it. In the thirty seconds it takes, the reaction loses its edge, and you have given yourself a tiny gap between the trigger and whatever comes next.

The ordinariness is the point. A big ritual is hard to reach for in a hot moment. A glass of water is not. It is boring, easy, and always available, which makes it something you will actually do.

So keep a few of these small resets in your pocket. A glass of water. A step outside. A slow stretch. None of them is impressive. All of them buy the same precious thing, a pause, and the pause is where the whole difference is made.
