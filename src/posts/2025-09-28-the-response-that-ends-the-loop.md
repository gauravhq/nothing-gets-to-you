---
title: "The response that ends the loop"
date: 2025-09-28
pageDescription: "A loop runs only while both people feed it. One calm, unexpected, off-script line is usually the exit."
---

Some exchanges become loops. They push, you push back, they escalate, you escalate, round and round, and nobody can find the exit. The loop feeds on matched energy.

There is a kind of response that ends it. Something that does not add to the spiral. An acknowledgment. You know what, you have a point. A step back. I do not want to fight with you. A calm refusal to continue. Let us come back to this later. Each one breaks the pattern the loop needs to keep going.

The loop can only run if both people keep feeding it. The moment one person responds differently, calmly, unexpectedly, off the script, the loop loses its power and starts to unwind.

So when you notice you are in one of these loops, look for the response that ends it rather than the one that continues it. You do not have to win the loop. You have to leave it. And usually, one calm, unexpected line is all it takes to open the door out.
