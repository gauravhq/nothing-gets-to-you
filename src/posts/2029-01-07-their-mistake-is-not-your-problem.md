---
title: "Their mistake is not your problem"
date: 2029-01-07
pageDescription: "The heavy urge to rescue everyone from their own choices is a weight you were never meant to carry."
---

Here is a releasing thought. Another person's mistake is usually not your problem to solve.

We take on so much that is not ours. Someone is about to do something we think is unwise, and we feel the weight of it, the need to warn, to fix, to stop them. But adults get to make their own choices, and their mistakes are theirs to make and to learn from, not ours to prevent.

This is not coldness. You can care, and offer a thought if it is welcome. But the heavy sense that you must correct and rescue everyone from their own decisions is a burden you were never meant to carry, and it wears you down.

So set it down. Their mistake is not your responsibility. You can let people be wrong, let them choose their own path, and trust them to handle the results. It frees them to be adults, and it frees you from a weight that was never yours.
