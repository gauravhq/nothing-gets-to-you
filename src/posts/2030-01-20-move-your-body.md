---
title: "Move your body"
date: 2030-01-20
pageDescription: "A tense, sedentary body reacts more. A daily walk lowers your baseline before you make a single other choice."
---

A steady mind is not just built in the mind. It is built in the body too, and one of the surest ways to steady yourself is to move.

When you move regularly, walk, exercise, whatever suits you, your whole system runs calmer. The baseline tension drops. The reactivity comes down. You have more room between a trigger and your response, not because you thought about it, but because your body is simply less wound up to begin with.

We treat calm as a purely mental thing, but a tense, sedentary, restless body reacts more, and a moved, settled body reacts less. The two are connected more tightly than we admit.

So make movement part of your practice. It does not have to be intense. A daily walk does more for your steadiness than you would guess. When you feel wound up, move. When you want a calmer baseline, move regularly. The body carries a lot of your reactivity, and moving it is one of the most reliable ways to set that reactivity down.
