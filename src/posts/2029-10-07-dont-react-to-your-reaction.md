---
title: "Don't react to your reaction"
date: 2029-10-07
pageDescription: "The self-attack after a slip feels like responsibility. It is just another reaction, and it fixes nothing."
---

When you slip, watch for the second wave. The reaction to your reaction.

You lose your cool, and then, right behind it, comes the self-attack. I am so bad at this. What is wrong with me. I always ruin things. This second reaction feels like taking responsibility. It is not. It is just another reaction, this one aimed at yourself, and it does the same kind of damage.

The first reaction cost you a moment. The self-attack can cost you the whole day, and it fixes nothing. Beating yourself up does not undo the slip, and it does not make you less likely to slip again. If anything, it makes it worse, by leaving you depleted and discouraged.

So when the second wave comes, name it the same way you would name any reaction. That is me reacting to my reaction. Then set it down. You can take a slip seriously without turning on yourself. Own it, learn from it, and skip the part where you punish yourself for being human.
