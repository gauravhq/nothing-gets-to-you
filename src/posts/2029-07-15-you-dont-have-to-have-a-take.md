---
title: "You don't have to have a take"
date: 2029-07-15
pageDescription: "You are allowed to have no opinion on the thing everyone is arguing about. It saves an enormous amount of energy."
---

There is a quiet pressure online to have an opinion on everything. Every event, every controversy, every thing everyone is talking about, you are supposed to have a take, and share it.

You do not. You are allowed to have no opinion. To not weigh in. To let a whole storm of discourse pass by without adding your voice or even forming a view.

This is more freeing than it sounds. So much of our agitation comes from feeling we must process and judge and respond to every issue that scrolls past. But you cannot care meaningfully about everything, and pretending to just wears you out.

So give yourself permission to not have a take. Some things you will care about deeply and engage with fully. Most things you can simply let go by. I do not have a view on this, and I do not need one, is a perfectly good place to stand. It saves an enormous amount of energy you can spend on what actually matters to you.
