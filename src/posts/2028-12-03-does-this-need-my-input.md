title: "Does this need my input"
date: 2028-12-03

Here is a filter worth running before you speak into a situation. Does this actually need my input?

So often we jump in out of habit. We offer the opinion, the correction, the better way, without asking whether anyone needed it. The conversation was going fine. The person was not stuck. Our two cents added nothing except the sound of our own voice.

Running the filter changes things. Before you weigh in, pause and ask. Is my input needed here, or am I just used to giving it? A surprising amount of the time, the honest answer is that it is not needed at all.

And not weighing in is its own quiet gift. It lets others carry the moment. It keeps you from cluttering things with unrequested advice. So hold your input until it is actually useful. The world does not need your comment on everything, and holding it back is not silence, it is respect.
