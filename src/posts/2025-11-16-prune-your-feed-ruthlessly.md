title: "Prune your feed ruthlessly"
date: 2025-11-16
pageDescription: "If something reliably steals your calm and gives nothing back, you can cut it. A feed is a garden you tend."
---

You have more control over your feed than you use. You are allowed to prune it ruthlessly.

There are accounts, topics, and sources that reliably leave you agitated. You know which ones. The urge is to keep following them, to stay informed, to not miss out. But if something consistently steals your calm and gives you nothing back, you can simply cut it.

Unfollow. Mute. Block. Unsubscribe. None of this is hiding from reality. It is choosing what gets a direct line to your nervous system, which is a reasonable thing to protect.

So do an honest audit. What in your feed reliably ruins your mood? Then be ruthless. Remove it. You will not miss most of it, and the important things have a way of reaching you regardless. A curated feed is not an echo chamber. It is a garden you tend on purpose, keeping what nourishes and pulling what only leaves you tense. Your attention is yours. Guard the door.
