---
title: "When the insult is aimed at a group"
date: 2026-08-09
pageDescription: "Someone runs down everyone who thinks the way you do, without knowing you are one of them. Why that stings differently, and why no verdict was actually passed on you."
---

Someone is running down everyone who holds the opposite view. Not you, exactly. He does not know you are one of them. He is just talking, and the contempt lands anyway.

This stings in an odd way. Nobody addressed you, so there is nothing to answer. You were not accused, you were counted. And the pull that follows is strong: declare yourself, correct him, defend the people he just dismissed in one sentence.

Ask what would actually change. Someone who insults everyone on one side has already told you he is not open today. He is not weighing views. He is showing the room exactly which buttons work on him.

Then name what really stung, because it is usually not his opinion. It is being counted as something you are not, by someone who never asked.

See that clearly and it loosens. It is a statement about him. It is not a verdict on you, because he never looked long enough to pass one.

Nobody appointed you the defender of everyone he disagrees with. You are allowed to put that down.
