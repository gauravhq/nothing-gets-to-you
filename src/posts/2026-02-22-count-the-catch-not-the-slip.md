---
title: "Count the catch, not the slip"
date: 2026-02-22
pageDescription: "The pauses you took are the real progress, but they are invisible. Start counting the catches, not the slips."
---

We keep score all wrong. We count the times we lost it and ignore the times we caught it. That guarantees we feel like we are failing.

Think about a normal week. There were probably several moments where you paused, where you did not send the message, where you let something go that you would have reacted to before. Those catches are the actual progress. But they are invisible, because nothing happened, so you never notice them. Meanwhile the one time you slipped is loud, and it is all you remember.

So flip the scoreboard. Start counting the catches. The pause you took. The reply you did not fire. The provocation you let pass. Each one is a small win, and there are more of them than you think.

When you count the catches instead of the slips, two things happen. You see that you are actually improving, and the encouragement makes you more likely to keep going. Measure the right thing, and the progress becomes visible.
