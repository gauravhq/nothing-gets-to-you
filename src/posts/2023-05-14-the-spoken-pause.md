---
title: "The spoken pause"
date: 2023-05-14
pageDescription: "When you cannot walk away, you can still buy a few seconds out loud."
---

Some arguments happen live, out loud, face to face, and there is no drafts folder to save you.

Even then, you can build a small gap. Slow down. Take a breath before you answer. Say something honest and plain, like I do not want to answer this badly, give me a second. Ask a question instead of firing a reply.

Each of these buys a few seconds, and a few seconds is often all the difference between a reaction you regret and a response you can stand behind. The heat spikes fast, and it also falls fast if you do not feed it.

You will not always manage it. That is fine. But the spoken pause is always available, even in the middle of the room, even when you cannot leave. You are never as trapped in the moment as the moment wants you to feel.
