---
title: "When to speak and when to stay silent"
date: 2023-04-02
pageDescription: "The test is simple: can your words improve something that matters?"
---

Not reacting does not mean never speaking. The hard part is telling apart what to say from what to let go.

Here is a simple test. Speak when silence would let real harm or quiet resentment continue. Speak, too, when someone genuinely asks for your honest view. In those moments, saying nothing costs more than speaking.

The rest of the time, the question is whether your words can improve something that matters, or whether you only want to correct the record and feel right. If it is the second, the peace you keep by staying quiet is worth more than the point.

So the real skill is not silence, and it is not always speaking. It is learning, moment by moment, to spot the rare thing that must be said among the thousand things that only feel like they must.
