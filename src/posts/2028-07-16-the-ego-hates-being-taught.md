---
title: "The ego hates being taught"
date: 2028-07-16
pageDescription: "Good advice can sting because being taught brushes the ego. Look past the sting to the useful thing."
---

Notice how advice can sting, even good advice, even asked-for advice. That sting is the ego, which hates being taught.

Being given a tip, a correction, a better way, brushes against a quiet belief that you should already know. So instead of hearing the useful thing, you feel talked down to, and you bristle, and you miss the help that was offered.

This is worth seeing, because it quietly costs you. The person most allergic to being taught is the person who learns the slowest, not because they lack the ability, but because their ego keeps swatting the lessons away.

So when advice stings, pause on the sting itself. That is just my ego not liking to be taught. Then look past it to the actual content. Is it useful? Often it is. The ability to receive a correction without your pride flaring is one of the quiet skills behind everyone who keeps getting better.
