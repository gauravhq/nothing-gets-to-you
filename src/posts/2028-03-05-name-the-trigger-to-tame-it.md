---
title: "Name the trigger to tame it"
date: 2028-03-05
pageDescription: "Naming what set you off, even silently, takes a surprising amount of its power away."
---

There is a small move that takes power away from a trigger. You name it.

Something sets you off and the heat rises fast. But if you can pause and say, even silently, that is my old fear of being disrespected, or that is me being tired more than me being wronged, something shifts. The reaction stops running the show from the shadows.

Naming works because a trigger does most of its damage while it is invisible. You think you are just responding to the moment, when really an old pattern is pulling the strings. The moment you see it and name it, you are no longer fully inside it. You are looking at it.

You do not have to name it perfectly. Even a rough label helps. This is a trigger. This is bigger than the moment. That small act of naming turns the volume down, every time.
