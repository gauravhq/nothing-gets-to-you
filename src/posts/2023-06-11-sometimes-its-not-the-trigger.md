---
title: "Sometimes it is not the trigger"
date: 2023-06-11
pageDescription: "When your reaction seems too big for the moment, look at the day behind it."
---

Sometimes a small thing sets off a reaction that is far too big for it, and the trigger is not really the point.

You snap at a minor comment, and if you are honest, it was not the comment. It was the third hard thing that day, on four hours of sleep, after a week that has been quietly grinding you down. The comment just happened to be there when the pressure finally found a crack.

This is worth knowing, because it changes the fix. You do not always need to analyze the trigger. Sometimes you need to notice that you are depleted, be gentle about it, and take care of the thing underneath.

When a reaction feels out of proportion, ask what else is going on. Often the honest answer is not about the person in front of you at all. It is about how empty the cup already was.
