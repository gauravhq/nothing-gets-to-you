---
title: "The promise: be moved less"
date: 2030-03-10
pageDescription: "This does not promise you will feel less. It promises you will be moved less. Those are very different things."
---

It is worth being clear about what this promises, and what it does not. It does not promise you will feel less. It promises you will be moved less.

Those are very different. Feeling less would be a loss, a flattening, a life with the color drained out. That is not the aim, and it would not be worth having. The aim is to feel everything fully and still not be swept away by it. To be a person who cares deeply and stays steady, both at once.

So this is not a promise of a numb, unbothered life. It is a promise of a steadier one. Not a quieter world, because the world will keep giving you reasons to react. A quieter you, meeting that world with more steadiness than it can shake.

Be moved less, not because you have stopped caring, but because you have learned to hold what you feel without letting it run you. That is the whole promise, and it is enough.
