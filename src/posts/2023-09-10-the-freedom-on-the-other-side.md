---
title: "The freedom on the other side"
date: 2023-09-10
pageDescription: "The less you need to be right, the more peace you get to keep."
---

There is a strange gift hiding in all of this. The less you need to be right, the more peace you get to keep. It is almost a straight trade.

A person who needs less agreement is harder to disturb, because fewer disagreements feel personally threatening. And when they let an argument go, they have not backed down. They chose, and choosing takes a strength the one who kept pushing does not have.

We imagine that needing to win makes us strong. Usually it makes us fragile, at the mercy of anyone who disagrees. The real strength is being able to hear you are wrong, or you see it differently, and stay steady.

That is the freedom on the other side of not needing to be right. Not a smaller life, a larger one, where far fewer things can reach in and take your calm without your permission.
