---
title: "Respond to the person, not the jab"
date: 2029-04-22
pageDescription: "A sharp jab almost always comes from someone having a hard time. Answer the human, not the weapon."
---

When someone comes at you sharp, there are two things you can respond to. The jab, or the person behind it.

Respond to the jab and you are in a fight, matching their sharpness, escalating, both of you swinging. Respond to the person, the tired, stressed, hurting human who threw the jab, and everything changes. You are no longer fighting the words. You are meeting the one who said them.

Because a sharp jab almost always comes from a person having a hard time. Hurt people are prickly. Stressed people snap. The jab is the symptom. The struggling person is the cause.

So when the sharp thing comes, try to look past it. What is going on with them? You do not have to excuse the behavior. You just choose to respond to the human instead of the weapon. It defuses more fights than any comeback, because it answers what is actually there, not just what was thrown.
