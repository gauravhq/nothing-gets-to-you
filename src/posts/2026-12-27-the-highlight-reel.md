---
title: "The highlight reel"
date: 2026-12-27
pageDescription: "You are comparing your whole messy life to everyone else's edited best moments."
---

Not all of what a screen does to you is anger. Some of it is quieter, and just as corrosive.

You scroll past someone's holiday, someone's promotion, someone's perfect-looking family, and something tightens. Not rage. A small, sour envy. A sense that everyone else is doing better and you are somehow falling behind.

But you are comparing your whole messy inside to everyone else's edited outside. A feed is a highlight reel, curated on purpose. It cannot fairly tell you whether your life is ahead or behind.

The move is the same as with anger. Notice the feeling, name where it came from, and remember the feed may have amplified it. Then put the phone down and return to your actual life, the one with texture and truth in it, being lived quietly where most of the good things happen.
