---
title: "The last word is a trap"
date: 2024-04-21
pageDescription: "Chasing the final line keeps you in the ring. Letting it go is how you step out."
---

There is a strong pull to have the last word. To land the final line, the one that settles it. It feels like winning.

It is a trap. The last word keeps you in the ring. As long as you need it, you are still fighting, still tense, still handing the other person power over whether you feel done. And they can always say one more thing, so the last word is never quite yours.

Letting it go is how you actually step out. Not because they were right, but because you no longer need the exchange to end on your line. You are free to let it just end.

Try it once. Let them have the final word. Notice that nothing bad happens, and that you feel lighter for not needing it. The last word costs more to win than it is ever worth to have.
