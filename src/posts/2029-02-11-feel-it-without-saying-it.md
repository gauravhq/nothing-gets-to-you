---
title: "Feel it without saying it"
date: 2029-02-11
pageDescription: "You can feel a thing fully and still choose not to voice it now. That is not swallowing, it is a choice."
---

Here is a skill worth building. Feeling something fully without having to say it.

We often think a feeling has to come out, or it is being suppressed. But there is a middle path. You can feel the anger, the hurt, the frustration, all the way, and still choose not to voice it right now. The feeling is honored. It just is not acted on.

This is not swallowing. Swallowing is pretending you do not feel it. This is the opposite, fully admitting the feeling to yourself, and then deciding, freely, what to do with it, which is sometimes nothing.

So when a strong feeling rises, try letting it be there without rushing it out of your mouth. Yes, I am angry. I feel this. And I do not have to say it to anyone this second. The feeling is real and it is allowed. Whether it becomes words is a separate choice, and it is yours.
