---
title: "The ten-second pause"
date: 2023-01-08
pageDescription: "The plainest tool there is. Before you respond, give it ten seconds."
---

The simplest tool in the whole book is also the plainest. Before you respond to something that stings, give it ten seconds.

That is it. You do not have to be wise in those ten seconds. You do not have to solve anything. You just have to not fire yet.

Take a slow breath. Let the first, hottest version of your reply come and go, because it always comes, and it is almost never the one you want to send. In ten seconds the spike of heat starts to drop on its own, and a steadier version of you gets a chance to speak.

Most of the damage we do is done inside the first few seconds. The pause is just you refusing to hand those seconds the pen.
