---
title: "The if-then plan"
date: 2026-07-26
pageDescription: "Pair a likely trigger with a chosen response in advance, so the calm you decides instead of the hot you."
---

You cannot count on being wise in the hot moment, so decide beforehand. That is what an if-then plan is. You pair a likely trigger with a chosen response, in advance.

If my boss sends a cold email, then I wait an hour before replying. If that relative brings up the topic, then I breathe and change the subject. If a meeting gets heated, then I say I will come back to it. You write the plan while you are calm, so that when the trigger comes, the response is already decided.

This works because it removes the need for willpower in the moment. You are not trying to summon your best self under pressure. You are just following a plan your calm self already made.

So look at your known triggers, the ones that come up again and again, and make an if-then for each. When this happens, I will do that. Decided in advance, the response almost happens on its own, and the trigger loses most of its power to catch you.
