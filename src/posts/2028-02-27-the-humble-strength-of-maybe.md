---
title: "The humble strength of maybe"
date: 2028-02-27
pageDescription: "Maybe you are right is not surrender. It is a strong, steadying thing to be able to say."
---

There is a small phrase with a lot of power in it. Maybe you are right.

We treat it like surrender, like giving ground. It is the opposite. It takes real steadiness to say it. A fragile person cannot, because their sense of themselves is riding on the argument. Only someone secure can hand over a piece of the ground and stay perfectly fine.

And it changes everything in the room. The heat drops. The other person, no longer braced for a fight, softens too. A conversation that was stuck suddenly has somewhere to go.

You are not lying when you say it. You genuinely might be missing something. Maybe you are right leaves room for that, and for them, and for the friendship to matter more than the point.

Try keeping it in your pocket. It is not a white flag. It is quiet strength, and people feel it as strength.
