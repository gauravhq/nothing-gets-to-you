---
title: "Repair with yourself"
date: 2027-04-04
pageDescription: "The last repair after a reaction is often the one you owe yourself."
---

After a hard reaction, there is usually a repair to make with the other person. And there is one more, easy to skip: the repair with yourself.

On the drive home, the old voice starts up. You made it worse. You should have handled that better. That is the shame reaction, aimed inward, and it deserves the same steady answer you would give a friend.

You did a hard thing, imperfectly, which is what doing hard things looks like. You caught it. You are learning it. That is not failure. That is what learning a skill in real time actually is.

Being kind to yourself here is not letting yourself off the hook. It is staying steady enough to learn from the slip instead of drowning in it. You cannot build a calmer life on a foundation of quietly hating yourself for every reaction. Own it, learn, and be gentle.
