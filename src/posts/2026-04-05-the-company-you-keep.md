---
title: "The company you keep"
date: 2026-04-05
pageDescription: "You catch calm and agitation from the people around you. Lean toward the ones who steady you."
---

You catch calm and you catch agitation, both from the people around you. The company you keep shapes your steadiness more than you notice.

Spend time with people who are always outraged, always in drama, always reacting, and you will find yourself pulled into the same state, tense and reactive without quite knowing why. Spend time with steady people, and their calm rubs off too. Moods are contagious, and you absorb the ones you are around most.

This is worth being intentional about. You cannot choose everyone in your life, but you have more say than you use over who you spend your free time with, whose energy you sit in.

So notice how different people leave you. Who winds you up? Who settles you down? Lean toward the ones who steady you, and give a little less of yourself to the ones who reliably stir you up. The company you keep is quietly one of the biggest forces on your calm, and choosing it well makes steadiness far easier to hold.
