The event was quick. The replay is where the real cost lives.

Someone said the thing once. In the world, it lasted a few seconds. But in your head you play it again, and again, each time adding the perfect comeback, the fresh outrage, the case for why you were wronged. By the end you have lived it forty times.

That is the replay tax. You pay it not for what happened, but for how many times you choose to happen it again.

And here is the odd thing. The replay changes nothing out there. The other person is not in the room. Nobody hears your brilliant comeback. You are the only one still paying.

When you catch the replay starting, name it. This already happened. I am the only one still here. Then step out. The event you cannot undo. The replay you can stop.
