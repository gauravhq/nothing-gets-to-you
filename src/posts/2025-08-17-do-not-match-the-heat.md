---
title: "Do not match the heat"
date: 2025-08-17
pageDescription: "A fire needs fuel. When one person refuses to add heat, the other person's heat has nowhere to go."
---

When someone brings heat, every instinct says to match it. They raise their voice, so you raise yours. They get sharp, so you get sharper. It feels like standing up for yourself. It just pours fuel on the fire.

The stronger move is to not match the heat. Stay level while they climb. Keep your voice steady while theirs rises. It feels almost unnatural at first, like you are giving ground. You are not. You are refusing to feed the escalation.

And it works. A fire needs fuel. When one person will not add heat, the other person's heat has nowhere to go, and it usually starts to come down, because it is hard to keep shouting at someone who is calm.

So when the heat comes, do the opposite of the instinct. Go a little slower, a little quieter, a little steadier. You are not losing by not matching them. You are quietly taking the fire out of the whole exchange.
