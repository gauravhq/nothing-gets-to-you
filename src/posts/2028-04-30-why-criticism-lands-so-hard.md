---
title: "Why criticism lands so hard"
date: 2028-04-30
pageDescription: "Feedback stings more than the words alone because it lands on old ground, not just the point made."
---

Criticism almost always stings more than the words alone would explain. A small note about your work, and your whole day tilts. Why does it land so hard?

Because it rarely lands on fresh ground. It lands on old ground, on every time you felt not good enough, on the fear that you are being found out. The person meant one small thing. Inside you, it hit a much older, more tender spot.

Seeing this helps, because it separates the two. There is the actual feedback, which is usually smaller and more useful than it felt. And there is the old ache it brushed, which is not really about this note at all.

So when criticism knocks you sideways, pause and split it. What was actually said? And what old wound did it touch? Often the real feedback is something you can use, and the sting is something you can set down, once you see they were never the same thing.
