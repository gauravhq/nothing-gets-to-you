title: "Outrage is the product"
date: 2025-10-12
pageDescription: "The feed makes money when you stay, and nothing keeps you scrolling like being upset. Your anger is the goal."
---

It helps to understand what the feed is actually selling. It is selling your outrage, and you are both the customer and the product.

The platforms make money when you stay, and nothing keeps you scrolling like being upset. Anger holds attention better than calm. So the machine learns, quietly, that the way to keep you is to keep feeding you things that make you react. Your outrage is not a side effect. It is the goal.

This is worth knowing, because it changes how you read that surge of anger at what you just saw. It was not an accident that you saw it. Something worked very hard to put the most enraging thing in front of you, because your reaction is what it runs on.

So when the feed lights you up, name it. This is the machine doing its job. Seeing the game does not make you immune, but it loosens the grip, and it makes the outrage a little easier to set down.
