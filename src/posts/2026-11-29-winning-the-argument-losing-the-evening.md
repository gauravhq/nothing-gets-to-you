---
title: "Winning the argument, losing the evening"
date: 2026-11-29
pageDescription: "You can win the exchange and still lose the night to it."
---

You can win the exchange and lose the evening. It happens more than we admit.

You make the point. You are right. And then you carry it home, replay it, snap at someone who had nothing to do with it, and lie awake with it. The scoreboard says you won. The night says otherwise.

Relationships do not run on who was right. They run on how safe people feel around you. Win enough arguments at home and you can find yourself alone with a very good record.

So before you go for the win, ask what it will actually cost. Sometimes the point is worth making. Often it is not, and the calm you keep by letting it go is worth more than the last word ever was.
