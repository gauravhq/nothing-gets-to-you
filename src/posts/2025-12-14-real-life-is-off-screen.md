title: "Real life is off screen"
date: 2025-12-14
pageDescription: "The person across the table is more real than anyone in the feed. Lift your eyes and return to the life that is here."
---

It is worth remembering, often, where your actual life is happening. It is not on the screen. It is off it.

The screen can start to feel like the main event, the place where the real things happen, where you must keep up. But your real life is the people in the room with you, the meal in front of you, the work of your hands, the walk outside. All of that is off-screen, and it is quietly the whole point.

The machine wants you to believe the opposite, that what is on the feed is urgent and what is around you can wait. It has that backwards.

So lift your eyes now and then. The person across the table is more real than anyone in the feed. The evening you are actually in matters more than any event you are watching from a distance. Put the screen down and return to the life that is right here, off-screen, where the good things actually live and where your peace has always been.
