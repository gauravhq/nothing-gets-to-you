---
title: "The email you don't send"
date: 2022-11-13
pageDescription: "The most important moment of your workday is the one where you don't hit send."
---

There is a small, ordinary moment that quietly shapes your working life: the cursor hovering over send, on a reply you wrote while your chest was still tight.

Two futures live in that moment. In one, you send it. You win the exchange, and then spend three weeks in a low, cold war with someone you sit near every day. In the other, you do not send it. You save the draft. You get a glass of water. You come back later with a clearer head and write something shorter and more useful, and the whole thing is smaller by the end of the day.

The heat wants the first one. It always does. It feels urgent, and right, and overdue.

So make the rule before you are hot. Anything written in anger waits until morning. Not forever, just until the version of you that is calm has had a look. That one habit, kept in the one place you need it most, will save you more grief than almost anything else you do.
