---
title: "Right in the wrong tone"
date: 2024-05-19
pageDescription: "Being right rudely still loses. How you say the true thing decides whether it lands at all."
---

You can be right and still get nowhere, because you were right in the wrong tone.

The fact was correct. The delivery was sharp, or smug, or impatient. And the other person, hearing the tone, stopped listening to the fact. Now they are defending themselves against how you said it, and the true thing you offered is lost.

This is worth remembering, because we act as if being right is enough. It is not. A truth delivered with contempt does not land. It just starts a new fight about respect.

The same point, said gently and without the edge, can be heard. Same words, almost, different tone, and suddenly they are with you instead of against you. Being right is only half of it. How you carry it is the half that decides whether it does any good at all.
