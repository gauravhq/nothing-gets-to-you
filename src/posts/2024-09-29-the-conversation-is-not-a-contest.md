---
title: "The conversation is not a contest"
date: 2024-09-29
pageDescription: "Treat talking as a competition and even winning leaves you more alone than when you started."
---

Somewhere along the way, many of us started treating conversation like a contest. Points for the best line. Points for being right. A quiet winner and loser by the end.

But a conversation is not a contest, and the moment you treat it like one, you lose the actual good of it. You stop listening and start waiting for your turn to score. You stop connecting and start competing. And even if you win, you are left more alone than when you started.

The better frame is that you are building something together, not tearing each other down. Their point is not an attack to counter. It is a piece to add to.

Try dropping the scorekeeping for one conversation. No winning, no landing the last clever thing. Just two people thinking together. It is a strange relief, and it is where the warmth in talking actually lives.
