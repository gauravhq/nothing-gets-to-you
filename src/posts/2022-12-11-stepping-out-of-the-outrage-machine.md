---
title: "Stepping out of the outrage machine"
date: 2022-12-11
pageDescription: "Some of what you scroll through is built to make you react. You are allowed to switch it off."
---

A lot of the heat in modern life does not come from the people around you. It comes from a screen designed to keep you reacting.

Outrage is engagement, and engagement is the product. Your anger, your need to reply, your urge to set a stranger straight, all of it feeds a machine that runs on exactly those reactions.

The stranger who wound you up is not your problem to solve. You will never meet them, and you will not change their mind.

What you can do is small and quiet. Turn off the notifications that pull you back. If you must swim in that water, do it on purpose, at a set time, and then get out. And when the group chat lights up, remember that far fewer things need your instant reply than the feed would have you believe.

Stepping out is not going numb to the world. It is keeping the calm you carry into the parts of your life that are actually yours.
