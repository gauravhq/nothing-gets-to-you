---
title: "The need to be right is fear"
date: 2028-01-30
pageDescription: "The hard push to be right is usually insecurity wearing armor. Seeing that loosens the grip."
---

The harder someone needs to be right, the more you are usually looking at fear, not confidence.

Think about it. A person who is genuinely secure can hear a challenge and stay easy. They can say, maybe, or I had not thought of that, without feeling shaken. It is the person who is unsure underneath who cannot bend, who has to win every point, who treats being wrong like a threat.

So when you feel that hard need to be right rising in you, look under it. Often it is not the truth you are defending. It is a shaky sense of yourself, dressed up as certainty.

Seeing that helps, because it turns down the urgency. You do not have to protect your worth by winning a point. Your worth is not on trial. And once you feel that, being right stops mattering nearly as much.
