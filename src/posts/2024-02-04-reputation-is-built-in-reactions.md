---
title: "Reputation is built in reactions"
date: 2024-02-04
pageDescription: "People forget who was right. They remember how you were when it got hard."
---

People do not remember who was right. They remember how you were.

Years later, nobody recalls the details of the disagreement. They recall that you stayed steady, or that you lost it. That you were fair under pressure, or that you turned sharp. Your reactions, more than your arguments, are what people carry away about you.

This is quietly one of the biggest costs of reacting. You can win the point and lose something far more valuable, the sense that you are someone safe to be around when things get hard.

Steadiness in a tense moment is remembered as strength, and it builds trust in a way no won argument ever does. So when the heat rises, know that more is at stake than this one exchange. You are showing people who you are. They will remember the how long after they have forgotten the what.
