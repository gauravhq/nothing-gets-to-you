---
title: "The emergency plan"
date: 2023-08-13
pageDescription: "For the big provocations, decide your steps now, while you are calm."
---

Most of this is for the small stuff, the thousand daily provocations that do not deserve your peace. But now and then something big lands, and it helps to have a plan made in advance.

When the provocation is large, calm does not mean doing nothing. It means calm action. Step one, do not respond from the hot version of you. Step two, write down what actually happened, plainly, while it is fresh. Step three, get advice through the right channel before you act. Step four, respond later, from the steady version of you, with the facts in hand.

Having these steps decided ahead of time means you are not improvising in the worst moment. You are following a path your calm self laid down.

Big or small, the principle holds. The heat does not get to make the important decisions. You do, once you have cooled enough to think.
