---
title: "Peace is quiet, not loud"
date: 2023-11-19
pageDescription: "Anger shouts for the stage. Peace just sits there, easy to overlook and easy to trade away."
---

Peace does not announce itself. That is part of why we miss it.

Anger is loud. Worry is loud. They demand the whole stage. Peace just sits there, quiet, easy to overlook. You tend to notice it only by what is absent. No tight chest. No argument running in your head. No urge to check, to fix, to fire back.

Because it is quiet, we forget to value it, and we trade it cheaply for the loud satisfaction of a good vent or a won point. The loud thing feels like more. It is not.

Try noticing peace the way you notice silence after noise. Oh. It is calm right now. Nothing is pulling at me. Naming it, even for a second, helps you want it more than the loud things that keep taking it from you.
