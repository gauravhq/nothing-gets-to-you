---
title: "You set the tone"
date: 2025-10-05
pageDescription: "Tone is contagious, and people take their cue from whoever is most grounded. That can be you."
---

In any exchange between two people, someone sets the tone. It might as well be you.

Tone is contagious. Meet sharpness with sharpness and the whole thing goes sharp. Meet it with steadiness and, more often than you would expect, the other person comes toward your calm. People take their cue from whoever is most grounded, and if that is you, you quietly lead the whole interaction somewhere better.

This is real power, and it is quiet. You do not control what the other person brings. You do control what you bring, and what you bring shapes the space between you more than you think.

So walk into the hard conversation knowing you can set the tone. Bring calm, and calm tends to spread. Bring steadiness, and it steadies the room. You are not at the mercy of the other person's mood. You are, in every exchange, holding one end of the tone, and how you hold it changes everything.
