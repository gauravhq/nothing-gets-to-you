---
title: "The reminder card"
date: 2023-08-27
pageDescription: "One line, carried in your pocket, for the moment the heat arrives."
---

Sometimes all you need in a hot moment is a single line to hold onto.

A reminder card, real or imagined, that you can reach for when the heat rises. Not a whole philosophy. One line. The reaction is short, the tail is long. Or, does this deserve my peace? Or simply, let it land, then choose.

The point of one line is that it fits in the gap. When you are flooded, you cannot run through a chapter of ideas. But you can hold a single sentence, and a single sentence is often enough to buy the pause where a better choice can form.

Pick a line that lands for you, and keep it close. When the moment comes, you will not have time to think it all through. You will just need one true thing to grab, to steady yourself long enough to not react.
