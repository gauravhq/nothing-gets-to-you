---
title: "A thirty-day start"
date: 2023-07-23
pageDescription: "You do not need a new life. You need a small, repeatable start."
---

You do not need to overhaul your whole life to become a steadier person. You need a small start you can actually keep.

Here is one. For thirty days, pick the single place you fire too fast, most likely work messages, and make one rule for it. Anything written in anger waits until morning. That is the whole plan.

Thirty days is long enough for a rule to stop feeling like effort and start feeling like how you do things. You will forget some days. That is fine. You begin again the next day. That is the practice.

Grand resolutions tend to collapse under their own weight. A small, repeatable choice, kept in one spot, quietly changes more. Steadiness is built the way anything solid is built, slowly, in ordinary days, one small choice not to react at a time.
