---
title: "Mine the reaction for the lesson"
date: 2029-09-23
pageDescription: "A reaction you already had is information. Studied calmly afterward, with curiosity, it teaches you your own wiring."
---

A reaction you have already had is not just a failure. It is information, if you are willing to look.

After you lose it, once the heat has passed, there is a small window to learn. What set me off? What was I really reacting to? Was I tired, hungry, already stretched thin? What would I do differently next time? Each reaction, studied calmly afterward, teaches you something about your own wiring.

Most people skip this. They react, feel bad, and move on, and so they react the same way next week, having learned nothing. The ones who get steadier are the ones who turn around and study the reaction instead of just regretting it.

So mine it. Not with shame, with curiosity. Treat the reaction as a message about where your buttons are and what state left you vulnerable. The reaction already cost you something. The least you can do is collect the lesson it paid for, so the next one is a little less likely.
