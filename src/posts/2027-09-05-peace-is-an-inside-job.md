---
title: "Peace is an inside job"
date: 2027-09-05
pageDescription: "If your calm waits for the world to line up, you have handed the keys to things you cannot control."
---

We keep trying to arrange the world so we can finally relax. When this project ends. When that person changes. When things settle down.

But the world never fully cooperates, and even when it does, the calm does not last, because the next thing is already coming. If your peace depends on everything outside lining up, you have handed the keys to things you cannot control.

Steadiness is an inside job. It comes from how you meet what happens, not from finally getting the world to behave. That is not bad news. It is freedom. It means you do not have to wait for perfect conditions that will never arrive.

You can be steady in an unsettled world, because the steadiness was never out there to begin with. It was in how you hold things. And that part, unlike the world, is actually yours.
