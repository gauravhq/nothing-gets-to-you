title: "The ache of being ignored"
date: 2024-08-18

Some triggers are loud. This one is quiet. The specific ache of being ignored.

The message left on read. The idea in the meeting that nobody picks up. The feeling of being talked over, passed by, unseen. It does not always look like a big provocation, but it can set off a surprisingly strong reaction, a cold hurt that turns into resentment or a sharp need to be noticed.

It helps to name it for what it is. This is the being ignored trigger. Because once you see it, you can catch what it makes you do, the passive jab, the withdrawal, the trying too hard, and choose differently.

And often the story you tell yourself, that they ignored you on purpose, is not even true. People are busy and distracted, not cruel. Naming the ache keeps it from writing a story that turns a missed message into a wound it was never meant to be.
