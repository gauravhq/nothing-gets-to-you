---
title: "Margin in your day"
date: 2029-12-30
pageDescription: "A packed, back-to-back day makes you brittle. A little empty space absorbs the disruptions instead of you."
---

A packed, back-to-back day makes you brittle. A little margin makes you steady. The difference is the empty space.

When every minute is scheduled, with no gap between things, any small disruption throws the whole day off, and you react to it, because you have no slack to absorb it. A late train, a delayed meeting, a spilled coffee, and you are undone, not because it was big, but because there was no room for it.

Margin fixes this. A few empty pockets in the day, a buffer between things, some time that is not spoken for. With margin, disruptions land in the space instead of on you. You have room to absorb them without reacting.

So build a little margin in. Do not schedule every minute. Leave gaps. Arrive early. Give things more time than they strictly need. The empty space is not wasted. It is the cushion that keeps small problems from becoming reactions. A life with margin is a life with far more calm, because it has room to breathe when things go sideways.
