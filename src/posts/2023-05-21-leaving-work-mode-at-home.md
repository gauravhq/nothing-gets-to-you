---
title: "Leaving work mode at home"
date: 2023-05-21
pageDescription: "The heat from the day does not have to walk through your front door with you."
---

One of the quieter costs of reacting is what we carry home.

The cold email, the meeting where you were talked over, the colleague who took the credit. You leave the office, but the heat comes with you, and it lands on the people who had nothing to do with any of it.

There is a small ritual worth building: a moment, on the way home, to set the day down. A walk, a few minutes in the car before you go in, a slow breath at the door. Not to bury the day, but to decide, on purpose, that its reactions do not get to run your evening.

The work problems will still be there tomorrow, and you will handle them better having actually rested from them. Protecting your peace sometimes means not letting one part of your life set fire to the rest.
