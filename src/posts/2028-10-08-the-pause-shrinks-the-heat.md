---
title: "The pause shrinks the heat"
date: 2028-10-08
pageDescription: "A reaction surges and, left unfed, falls just as fast. The pause simply stops you feeding it at the peak."
---

Here is why the pause works, in one line. The heat spikes fast, and it falls just as fast, if you do not feed it.

A reaction is not a steady state. It surges, peaks, and then, left alone, it drops. The problem is that we feed it right at the peak, with a sharp reply or a stewing replay, and the feeding keeps it burning long past when it would have died on its own.

The pause simply stops the feeding. You wait, and the surge passes through, and a minute later the same situation feels smaller, more manageable, less urgent. Nothing changed out there. The wave just crested and fell.

So trust the pause to do the shrinking for you. You do not have to force yourself calm. You have to not add fuel for a little while, and let the natural drop happen. Wait out the peak, and the heat shrinks itself down to a size you can actually handle.
