---
title: "Why being wrong feels like danger"
date: 2023-03-05
pageDescription: "Your body can treat being wrong like a threat. Knowing that changes how you hold it."
---

Notice what happens in your body the moment you might be wrong. The heat, the tightening, the rush to defend. For a second, being wrong can feel less like a mistake and more like a threat.

That is an old, fast part of you doing its job, guarding a version of you that needs to look strong. It is not really about the fact under discussion. It is about the feeling of being exposed.

Once you can see that, it loosens. Being wrong about a fact is not dangerous. It is just information. The people worth keeping do not think less of you for it, and often think more.

So when that heat arrives, name it for what it is. Not danger. Just the old guard, standing up out of habit. You can be a person who can be wrong without going to war.
