---
title: "The slip is not the end"
date: 2025-12-28
pageDescription: "One reaction does not erase weeks of steadiness. The people who get calm are the ones a slip did not talk into quitting."
---

You will slip. You will react when you meant to stay steady. And when you do, one thought matters most. The slip is not the end.

We treat a single reaction as if it undoes everything. I lost it, so all my progress is gone, so what is the point. That story is false, and it does more harm than the reaction did. One slip does not erase weeks of steadiness any more than one missed workout erases a year of fitness.

Progress in this is not about never slipping. Everyone slips. It is about the overall trend, which keeps improving even with slips scattered through it.

So when you react, do not let it become a reason to give up. It is one data point, not a verdict. Note it, learn what you can, and keep going. The people who get steady are not the ones who never slipped. They are the ones who did not let a slip talk them into quitting.
