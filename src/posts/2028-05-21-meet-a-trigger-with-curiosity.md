---
title: "Meet a trigger with curiosity"
date: 2028-05-21
pageDescription: "Instead of shame when you get triggered, try curiosity. Interest disarms what judgment only tightens."
---

When you get triggered, the usual second move is shame. There I go again. What is wrong with me. That reaction to the reaction only makes it worse.

Try curiosity instead. When the heat rises, get interested. Huh. That really got me. I wonder why. What was that about? Interest does something judgment never can. It creates a little space between you and the reaction, and in that space you can actually see what is going on.

Shame tightens. It makes you defensive, even with yourself, and it teaches you to look away from your own triggers. Curiosity opens. It lets you study the thing instead of hiding from it.

You are not a problem to be scolded. You are a person with some old, understandable wiring, worth getting to know. Meet your triggers the way you would meet a curious puzzle, and they slowly lose their grip, one interested look at a time.
