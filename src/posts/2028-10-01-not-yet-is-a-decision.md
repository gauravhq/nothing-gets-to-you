---
title: "Not yet is a decision"
date: 2028-10-01
pageDescription: "A pause is not failing to decide. Not yet is a real, often wise choice that keeps your options open."
---

We think of a pause as not deciding. It is a decision, and often the best one available. Not yet.

When you say not yet, you are not being weak or avoiding the issue. You are actively choosing to wait, which is a real and often wise choice. Not yet to the reply. Not yet to the yes. Not yet to the big reaction. You keep all your options open and let the heat drain before you commit.

The reactive mind hates not yet. It insists everything must be settled now, this second. But very little actually must, and the things pushed hardest to be decided now are exactly the ones that most benefit from a delay.

So let not yet be a full answer. You are not dodging. You are deciding to decide later, from a steadier place. Give the important things the dignity of a pause, and let not yet be the calm, deliberate choice that it is.
