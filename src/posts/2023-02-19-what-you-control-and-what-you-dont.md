---
title: "What you control and what you don't"
date: 2023-02-19
pageDescription: "A great deal of frustration comes from spending energy on what was never yours to move."
---

Focus on what is yours to choose. Your actions, your words, your responses, your attention. These you can move.

Other people, their opinions, the news, the past, most of what you spend the day worrying about. These you cannot. A great deal of avoidable frustration comes from pouring energy into the second group and calling it caring.

The practice is simple to say and hard to keep. When you feel yourself churning, ask which group the thing belongs to. If it is yours, act. If it is not, loosen your grip, again and again, without pretending you do not care.

You are not being cold. You are refusing to drain yourself on things that will not move no matter how hard you push. What is left, you get to put where it actually counts.
