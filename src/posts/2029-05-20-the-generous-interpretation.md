---
title: "The generous interpretation"
date: 2029-05-20
pageDescription: "For anything someone does there is a mean story and a generous one. The generous one is usually more accurate."
---

For almost anything someone does, there are two stories available. The mean one and the generous one. You usually get to choose which you believe.

The short reply. The mean story says they are being rude, they do not respect you. The generous story says they are busy, or stressed, or typing on a phone between meetings. Same reply, two very different reactions depending on which story you pick.

And the generous one is not just kinder. It is usually more accurate. Most people, most of the time, are not out to hurt you. They are distracted, tired, wrapped up in their own lives, and it has nothing to do with you.

So when something lands wrong, reach for the generous interpretation first. Assume the kinder reason until you have real proof otherwise. It is not naive. It is smart, because it saves you from a hundred reactions to slights that were never actually slights, just stories your mind rushed to tell.
