---
title: "Calm is not numb"
date: 2023-11-05
pageDescription: "Steadiness is not feeling less. It is feeling fully and not being run by it."
---

There is a fear that being calm means feeling nothing. Going flat. Caring less. It does not.

Calm is not numb. A numb person has switched off. A calm person feels the whole thing, the anger, the grief, the sting, and is simply not run by it. The feeling is fully there. It just does not grab the wheel.

This matters, because if you think steadiness means killing your feelings, you will resist it, and you should. A life with the feeling drained out is not the goal.

The aim is to feel deeply and act wisely, both at once. To be moved by things and still choose your response. You are not trying to want less or care less. You are trying to keep your hands on the wheel while the feeling rides along. That is calm. Not cold. Awake.
