---
title: "Two kinds of silence"
date: 2026-08-16
pageDescription: "One silence protects you. Another one slowly poisons you. They can look identical."
---

Not all silence is the same, and the difference matters.

There is a silence that comes from fear. You feel something, you shove it down, and you tell yourself you are being mature. But it does not leave. It sits there, and it leaks out later, as coldness, or a snapped remark about something unrelated.

And there is a silence that comes from choice. You could speak. You know exactly what you would say. And you decide, freely, that this one is not worth it, with a clear head and a settled feeling, not a swallowed one.

From the outside they can look identical, two people saying nothing. Inside, they are opposites.

Here is a simple check. When you find yourself going quiet, ask one question. Am I quiet because I am afraid, or because I have chosen this? Do not judge the answer. Just name it. If it is fear, you have found something worth saying, later and calmly. If it is choice, rest easy.
