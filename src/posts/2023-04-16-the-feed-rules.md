title: "The feed rules"
date: 2023-04-16
pageDescription: "You are allowed to shape what you let into your head every day."
---

You are allowed to choose what you let into your head.

Much of the heat in modern life is delivered, on purpose, by feeds designed to keep you reacting. You do not have to accept the whole stream just because it is there.

A few plain rules help. Turn off the notifications built to pull you back. Unfollow the accounts that reliably leave you agitated. If you must swim in that water, do it at a set time, and then get out. Notice which inputs steal your peace, and take less of them.

This is not hiding from the world. It is refusing to let a machine set the temperature of your mind for you. What you consume becomes what you carry, and you have more say over that than the endless scroll would like you to believe.
