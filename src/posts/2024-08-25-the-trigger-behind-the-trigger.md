---
title: "The trigger behind the trigger"
date: 2024-08-25
pageDescription: "What sets you off on the surface often stands in front of a deeper thing you are really reacting to."
---

Sometimes the thing that set you off is not really the thing you are reacting to. There is a trigger behind the trigger.

You snap because someone was late. But dig a little and it was not the lateness. It was feeling unimportant, like your time does not matter to them. The lateness was the surface. The real trigger was the meaning you gave it.

This is worth doing, because if you only ever address the surface, you keep getting caught by the same deeper thing wearing new clothes. Late today, interrupted tomorrow, overlooked next week, all of it hitting the same buried spot.

So when a reaction feels strong, ask the second question. Not just what happened, but what did it mean to me? What was I really reacting to? The honest answer often points past the moment to something older and deeper, which is exactly the thing worth tending.
