---
title: "The power of the pause"
date: 2026-07-26
pageDescription: "Almost the whole skill is buying yourself the second or two it takes to choose."
---

Almost the entire skill comes down to one small thing: the gap between what lands and what you do.

When the gap is zero, you are already reacting before you have decided anything. When the gap is even a little wider, something changes. A breath. A moment where you get to choose instead of just fire.

The good news is that the gap can be built. You do not have to be wise in your worst moment. You can decide, in a calm one, what you will do when the heat comes.

The simplest version: when a message stings, put the phone down and answer in an hour. When a meeting heats up, say you want to think about it and come back. The delay is not avoidance. It is you choosing the ground you stand on.

You will be surprised how few situations actually require an instant response, and how much steadier you feel the moment you stop giving them one.
