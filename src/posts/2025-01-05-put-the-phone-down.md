---

With a screen, the pause has a physical form. You put the phone down.

A message stings, a post enrages, and your thumbs are already moving to reply. The screen makes reacting frictionless, instant, one tap away. So the pause has to be just as physical. Put the phone down. Set it on the table, face down, and step back.

That small act breaks the loop. Away from the screen, the urgency drains. The reply that felt so necessary a second ago starts to look optional, then unwise, then unnecessary. The distance does the work.

You do not have to be strong enough to resist typing while staring at the thing. You just have to be strong enough to put the phone down and walk to another room. The reply will still be possible in an hour, from a calmer you. Most of the time, by then, you will not want to send it at all. Down goes the phone, down goes the reaction.
