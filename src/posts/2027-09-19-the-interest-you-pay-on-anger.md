---
title: "The interest you pay on anger"
date: 2027-09-19
pageDescription: "Anger feels free in the moment. It is a loan, and the interest runs for days."
---

Anger feels free in the moment. It is not. It is a loan, and the interest is brutal.

You get the quick hit of firing back, of saying the sharp thing, of being right and loud. That is the money up front. Then the payments start. The replaying. The tension. The awkwardness you now have to smooth over. The sleep you lose. The trust you spent.

You keep paying, long after the moment that felt so satisfying is gone. The event lasted a minute. The interest runs for days.

So before you take the loan, ask if you can afford it. Sometimes the anger is worth the cost. Usually it is not, and the calm you keep by not borrowing is worth more than the quick hit ever was. Peace, it turns out, is the thing you buy by not spending on anger.
