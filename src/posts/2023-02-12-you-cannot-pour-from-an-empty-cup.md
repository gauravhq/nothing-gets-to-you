---
title: "You cannot pour from an empty cup"
date: 2023-02-12
pageDescription: "You are far more reactive when you are depleted. Your calm is not a fixed trait."
---

Here is a plain truth about reactions. You are far more reactive when you are depleted. The same comment that rolls off you on a good day can flatten you on a bad one.

Your ability to stay calm is not fixed. It rises and falls with how well you are looking after yourself. Sleep, food, rest, a little space. These are not soft extras. They are the ground your steadiness stands on.

This is oddly freeing. It means some of your worst reactions are not a character flaw. They are a tired, hungry, stretched-thin nervous system doing what tired systems do.

So part of the work is not in the hot moment at all. It is in the quiet choices that keep your cup from running empty, so that when something lands, you have something left to meet it with.
