---
title: "Wrong is not the same as harmful"
date: 2028-12-31
pageDescription: "Sort what you want to correct into wrong-and-harmless or wrong-and-harmful. Only the second is worth a word."
---

Not everything wrong is worth addressing, and the sorting question is simple. Is it wrong, or is it harmful?

A lot of wrong is harmless. A mistaken opinion about a movie. A shaky fact in a casual story. A view you think is silly but that hurts no one. These you can let go, because being wrong is not a crime, and the world is full of it.

Harmful is different. When something wrong is about to lead to a real bad outcome, hurt someone, or spread damage, that is worth a calm, clear word. That is when silence would cost more than speaking.

So when the urge to correct rises, run the sort. Wrong and harmless, let it go. Wrong and harmful, speak, gently. Most of what we itch to correct falls in the first pile. Learning to tell the two apart saves your energy for the corrections that actually matter, and spares everyone the ones that do not.
