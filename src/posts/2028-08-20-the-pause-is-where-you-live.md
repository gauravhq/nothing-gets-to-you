---
title: "The pause is where you live"
date: 2028-08-20
pageDescription: "The gap between what happens and what you do is small, and it is the one place you are actually free."
---

Between what happens and what you do, there is a gap. That gap is small, but it is where your whole freedom lives.

With no gap, you are not choosing anything. The event happens and the reaction fires, and you are just along for the ride. Widen the gap even a little, and suddenly there is room. Room to see the reaction coming. Room to decide. Room to be a person instead of a reflex.

Everything in this practice is really about that gap. The pause, the breath, the counting, the walk, all of it is just ways of making the space between trigger and response a bit wider.

So when you protect the pause, you are not just avoiding a bad reaction. You are claiming the one place where you are actually free. The gap is where you get to be you, on purpose. Guard it, widen it, and live there as much as you can.
