---
title: "Why being right so rarely brings peace"
date: 2026-07-12
pageDescription: "You can win the point and still lose the evening. Right and calm are not the same thing."
---

You can be completely right and still feel terrible.

You make your case. You have the facts. You win the exchange. And then you spend the rest of the night replaying it, or lying awake wishing you had said the clever thing you thought of too late.

Being right is about the fact. Peace is about you. They are not the same, and chasing the first almost never delivers the second.

Most arguments are not really about the point at all. Underneath, there is something older: the need to be seen, to not be dismissed, to not lose. The point is just the thing we fight over.

Try this. The next time you feel the pull to correct someone or win, make your case once, plainly, and then let it go. Not because you are wrong. Because your calm is worth more than the last word. You will notice, most of the time, that nothing falls apart, and your evening stays yours.
