---
title: "What to do after you lose it"
date: 2026-09-06
pageDescription: "You will react again. The repair matters more than the perfect record."
---

You are going to react. Even with all the practice, some days the heat gets there first. That is not a failure of the skill. It is just being a person.

What matters more than never slipping is what you do next.

The first move is to own it, plainly. Not a long, grovelling apology that makes the other person comfort you. Just the plain thing. I overreacted earlier. That was not fair to you. I am sorry. Then stop.

The second move is to learn the one thing. What got you? Which old bruise did that press? You are not doing this to punish yourself. You are doing it so the same button works a little less well next time.

And there is a repair to make with yourself too. When the shame starts up, telling you what is wrong with you, notice that this is just another reaction, aimed inward. Own it, learn from it, and walk on. The peace was never in getting it perfect. It was always in the coming back.
