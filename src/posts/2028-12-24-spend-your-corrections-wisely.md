---
title: "Spend your corrections wisely"
date: 2028-12-24
pageDescription: "Correct everything and you go broke, tuned out by the time something truly matters. Save your credit."
---

Think of your corrections like money. You have a limited amount of credibility and goodwill, and every correction spends a little of it.

If you correct everything, you go broke. People start to tune you out, brace around you, see you as the one who always has to be right. And then when something truly matters, when you really do need them to hear a correction, the account is empty. They do not listen, because you cried wrong too many times.

So spend your corrections wisely. Save them for what actually counts, the mistake that will cause real harm, the thing that truly needs setting straight. Let the small stuff go uncorrected, and keep your credit.

The person who corrects rarely, and only when it matters, gets listened to when they do. That is worth far more than the small satisfaction of being right about every little thing. Guard your account, and it will be there when you need it.
