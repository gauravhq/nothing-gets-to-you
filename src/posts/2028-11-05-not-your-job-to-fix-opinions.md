---
title: "Not your job to fix opinions"
date: 2028-11-05
pageDescription: "Chief corrector of wrong opinions is a job nobody gave you. You are allowed to resign."
---

Somewhere you may have picked up a job nobody gave you. Chief corrector of wrong opinions.

Someone says something you disagree with, and you feel responsible for fixing it. As if the world's accuracy depends on you speaking up. But it does not, and that self-appointed job is exhausting, and it rarely does any good.

Most opinions you feel the urge to correct are not causing harm. They are just different from yours. And the person did not ask to be corrected, and will not thank you for it, and will not change their mind because of it.

So you can quietly resign from the job. Not every wrong thing needs your fixing. Not every opinion you disagree with needs your comment. Letting people hold views you think are mistaken, when nothing important hangs on it, is not laziness. It is peace, and it frees up an enormous amount of energy you did not know you were spending.
