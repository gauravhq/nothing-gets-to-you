---
title: "Progress is not a straight line"
date: 2029-10-21
pageDescription: "Getting steadier looks like a jagged line trending up. Expect the dips. Measure the trend, not the down days."
---

If you expect a straight line of steady improvement, you will quit the first time you slip. Progress does not work that way.

Getting steadier looks more like a jagged line trending slowly upward. Good stretches, then a bad day. A week of calm, then a reaction that feels like starting over. Two steps forward, one step back, over and over. That is not failure. That is what real change looks like for everyone.

The mistake is to measure yourself by the down days and conclude you are getting nowhere. Zoom out and the trend is what matters, and the trend, with all its dips, keeps rising.

So when you have a bad day, or a bad week, do not read it as proof that you are not changing. Read it as one dip in a line that is still climbing. Expect the setbacks. Build them into your picture of progress. The people who make it are not the ones who never dip. They are the ones who kept going through the dips.
