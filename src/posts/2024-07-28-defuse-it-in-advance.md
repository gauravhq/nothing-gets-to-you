---
title: "Defuse it in advance"
date: 2024-07-28
pageDescription: "You do not have to meet a known trigger cold. Knowing it is coming lets you disarm it beforehand."
---

Most of your triggers are not surprises. You know them. The family member who always brings up the one topic. The kind of feedback that always stings. The situation that reliably sets you off.

Because you know them, you can defuse them in advance. You do not have to walk in cold and hope. You can decide, while you are calm, how you want to handle it before it arrives. When she says that thing, I will breathe and let it pass. In that meeting, I will not answer the first jab.

A trigger disarms much more easily before it fires than after. In the moment, the heat is already up and choice is hard. Beforehand, calm and clear, you can set the plan.

So look at the week ahead. Where are the known triggers? Meet each one having already decided. Half their power is in catching you off guard, and you can take that away.
