---
title: "Certainty feels good, and can lie"
date: 2028-02-20
pageDescription: "Feeling sure is a pleasant feeling, and it is not the same as being right. Hold it a little loosely."
---

Feeling certain is a good feeling. It is also not the same as being right, and it fools us constantly.

When you are sure, everything lines up. The other side looks obviously wrong, your view looks obviously correct, and it seems strange that anyone could disagree. But that feeling of sureness is just a feeling. You have felt it before about things you later found out you had completely wrong.

This is worth holding onto, because certainty is what makes us push hardest, and it is exactly when we push hardest that we are least willing to check ourselves.

So treat your own certainty with a little suspicion. Not doubt everything, just hold it loosely. The strong feeling of being right is not proof. Some of the calmest, wisest people are the ones who can be sure and still leave a door open, in case the good feeling is lying again.
