Here is a rule worth adopting whole. When a message stings, you answer in an hour, not a second.

The damage in written exchanges happens in the first minute, when the sting is fresh and the reply is hottest. The hour rule simply removes that window. You feel the sting, you note it, and you decide to respond later, once the heat has drained.

An hour is usually enough. In that time, the spike falls, the perfect angry reply loses its appeal, and a steadier response becomes possible. Often you find you do not need to reply at all, or that a single calm line will do where a paragraph of heat was about to go.

So make it a standing rule, decided in advance so you do not have to summon the willpower in the moment. Stinging message, one hour minimum. Write the hot reply if you must, but do not send it for an hour. Almost every time, the you from an hour later will thank you.
