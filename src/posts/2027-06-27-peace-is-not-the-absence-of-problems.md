---
title: "Peace is not the absence of problems"
date: 2027-06-27
pageDescription: "A peaceful life is not one where nothing goes wrong. It is one where you do not go wrong with it."
---

Peace is not a life with no problems. If that were the bar, none of us would ever reach it.

The bills still come. People are still difficult. Plans still fall apart. A peaceful life is not one where none of that happens. It is one where those things happen and you do not come apart with them.

So stop waiting for the calm season, the one where nothing is wrong, before you let yourself feel steady. It is not coming. There is always something.

The steadiness this points at is not the absence of storms. It is the ability to stand in one without being swept off your feet. You can have a hard day and a settled mind at the same time. That is the whole skill, and it is available in the middle of the mess, not after it.
