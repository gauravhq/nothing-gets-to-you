---
title: "Daily habits for a steady mind"
date: 2023-07-30
pageDescription: "A handful of small, repeatable things do more than any grand resolution."
---

A handful of small, repeatable things, done most days, do more for your steadiness than any grand resolution.

A little stillness: some minutes with no screen, no input, no one to answer. A little looking back: revisiting a moment you reacted and asking, gently, what happened there. And less of whatever reliably makes you reactive, the feeds and the fights that leave you agitated.

None of these is dramatic. That is the point. You are not trying to transform yourself in a weekend. You are lowering the daily temperature, so that when something lands, you have more room to meet it.

You will have good days and bad days. And slowly, over months, a few more good ones, until the bad days become the exception. That is the real change, not a trick you perform in the moment, but a life quietly arranged to make reacting less likely.
