---
title: "The pause buys a better you"
date: 2025-02-02
pageDescription: "In the first hot second your worst self speaks. Wait a beat, and a wiser one arrives to take its place."
---

The best reason to pause is who shows up in the gap. A wiser version of you.

In the first hot second, the you that wants to speak is your worst self, the reactive, defensive, sharpest one. Wait a beat, and a different you arrives, the one who can see the whole picture, who remembers what matters, who knows how to say the hard thing kindly. That better self is real. It just runs a little slower than the reflex.

The pause is simply the time it takes for the better you to get to the front. Skip the pause and the worst you speaks. Take it, and the wiser one does.

So think of the pause not as holding back, but as waiting for reinforcements. The calm, clear, generous version of you is on the way. Give it the few seconds it needs to arrive, and let it, not the reflex, be the one who speaks.
