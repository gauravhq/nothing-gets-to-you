---
title: "The relief of not weighing in"
date: 2025-03-30
pageDescription: "Let one thing pass that you would normally comment on, and feel the small, real freedom of not having to."
---

There is a lightness you feel the first time you deliberately do not weigh in. A conversation goes by, someone says something you disagree with, and you simply let it pass. And nothing bad happens. And you feel oddly free.

We carry a low-level sense of duty to comment on everything, to register our view, to make sure the record reflects our take. Setting that duty down, even once, is a relief you have to feel to believe. All that energy you would have spent on the correction, the debate, the follow-up, you get to keep.

So try it on purpose. Pick one thing today that you would normally correct or comment on, and just let it go by. Notice the small freedom of not being the one who had to speak.

The more you practice it, the lighter your days get. Not everything needs your input, and not weighing in is quietly one of the most peaceful choices there is.
