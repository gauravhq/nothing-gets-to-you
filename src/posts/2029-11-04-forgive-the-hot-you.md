---
title: "Forgive the hot you"
date: 2029-11-04
pageDescription: "The version of you that reacted was flooded and running on old wiring. It is not your enemy. It is still learning."
---

The version of you that reacted was not your best self. It was your hot self, flooded, running on old wiring. And that version deserves forgiveness too.

After a slip, it is easy to hold a grudge against yourself, as if the reactive you were a separate, worse person who keeps ruining things. But that hot self was doing the best it could in a flooded moment, with a nervous system that got hijacked. It is not your enemy. It is just the part of you that has not finished learning yet.

Forgiving it is not letting yourself off the hook. It is refusing to build your growth on self-hatred, which never works anyway. You do not become steadier by despising the parts of you that still slip. You become steadier by understanding them.

So forgive the hot you. It got flooded. It reacted. It is learning, like the rest of you. Meet it with the same patience you would offer a friend who lost their cool, and it will slip a little less each time.
