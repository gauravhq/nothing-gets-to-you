---
title: "The shame spiral is a reaction too"
date: 2027-03-21
pageDescription: "After you react, there is a second reaction, aimed at yourself. It does the same damage."
---

When you slip and react, there is often a second wave right behind it: the shame. The voice that says, I always do this, what is wrong with me.

Here is what is easy to miss. That second wave is just another reaction, aimed this time at yourself. And it does exactly what an outward reaction does. The first reaction cost you a moment. The shame can follow you for hours, and it repairs nothing.

So when the spiral starts, name it. This is a reaction too. You can meet it the same way you meet any other, by noticing it, not feeding it, and choosing what to do instead.

What helps is not beating yourself up. It is owning the slip plainly, learning the one thing it has to teach, and walking on. The peace was never in getting it perfect. It was always in the coming back.
