---
title: "Your triggers are old bruises"
date: 2022-10-30
pageDescription: "The thing that set you off is rarely just the thing in front of you."
---

When a small comment lands like a slap, it is worth asking why something so small hit so hard.

Usually it is not just the comment. It landed on a place that was already sore. A bruise you have carried for years, maybe decades. The remark just found it.

A handful of these do most of the damage in most lives. For some people it is feeling disrespected. For others it is criticism, or the quiet sting of being ignored. You will have your own. Two or three of them probably account for most of your reactions, and the rest barely touch you.

The point is not to feel bad about the bruises. Everyone has them. The point is to know exactly where yours are, so that the next time a thumb finds one, you get a half-second of recognition before the heat takes the wheel.

A quiet way to map yours: over the next week, when something gets you, finish the sentence, honestly, in your own head. When someone did that, I felt small, because. The part after because is where the real information is.
