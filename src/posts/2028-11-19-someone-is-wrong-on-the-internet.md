---
title: "Someone is wrong on the internet"
date: 2028-11-19
pageDescription: "The internet is infinite and always wrong somewhere. You could correct strangers forever and never make a dent."
---

There is a feeling almost everyone knows. You see a comment online that is wrong, and you feel you must respond. Must set it straight. Cannot just leave it there.

This is one of the great time and peace sinks of modern life. Someone is wrong on the internet, and you appoint yourself to fix it, and an hour later you are tense, no minds have changed, and the wrong comment is still there.

The internet is infinite. There will always be another wrong thing. You could spend your entire life correcting strangers and never make a dent, and lose your calm a hundred times a day trying.

So let this be the easiest one to release. A stranger being wrong online is not your responsibility, not your problem, and not worth a second of your peace. Scroll past. The wrong comment will survive without your reply, and you will keep an afternoon you would otherwise have lost.
