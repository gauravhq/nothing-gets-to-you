---
title: "The news is not your emergency"
date: 2029-09-09
pageDescription: "Everything is framed as breaking and urgent. You can be informed without treating every headline as a fire in your house."
---

The news is built to feel like an emergency. Almost none of it is yours.

Everything is breaking, urgent, alarming, happening now. The tone is designed to grip you, to make each story feel like something you must attend to this instant. And so you carry a constant, low hum of emergency, reacting all day to events you cannot touch.

But most of what the news presents as your emergency is not. It is happening far away, or it is out of your hands, or it is simply not the crisis the framing makes it seem. You can be informed without treating every headline as a fire in your own house.

So hold the news at a healthy distance. Stay aware of what matters to you, act where you actually can, and let go of the rest. The churn wants your constant alarm. You do not owe it. Very little of what the feed calls urgent is truly your emergency, and treating it all as such just keeps you reacting to a world you cannot control.
