title: "The hangover of a blowup"
date: 2027-11-21
pageDescription: "The morning after you lose it comes with a dull, heavy residue, and you did not even drink."
---

There is a particular feeling the morning after you lose it. A dull, heavy hangover, and you did not even drink.

The heat is gone now. What is left is the residue. The vague shame. The replaying of what you said. The knowledge that you have some smoothing over to do. The blowup felt powerful last night. This morning it just feels like a mess you made.

That gap between the reaction and the morning after is worth remembering, because it is easy to forget in the heat. The moment promises release. It rarely mentions the hangover that follows.

So when the pull to blow up comes, try picturing tomorrow morning. The you who has to wake up and deal with it. That version of you almost never wishes you had let it rip. Far more often, they wish you had let it wait.
