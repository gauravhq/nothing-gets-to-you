---
title: "Deciding before you are hot"
date: 2023-04-30
pageDescription: "You cannot count on being wise in your worst moment. So decide in a calm one."
---

You cannot count on being wise in your worst moment. The heat is fast, and it does not wait for your better judgment.

So do the deciding earlier, when you are calm. Make the rule before you need it. Anything written in anger waits until morning. When a message stings, I answer in an hour, not a second. When a meeting heats up, I say I will come back to it.

A pre-decided rule turns the pause from something you have to summon into something that simply happens. You are not relying on your best self showing up in the worst second. You are letting your calm self, the one who made the rule, reach forward and protect you.

That is how steady people stay steady. Not by winning every hot moment, but by making fewer of the important decisions while they are hot.
