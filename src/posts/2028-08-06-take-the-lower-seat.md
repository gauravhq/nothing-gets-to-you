---
title: "Take the lower seat"
date: 2028-08-06
pageDescription: "The ego always grabs for the higher seat. Choosing the lower one is a strange, steadying relief."
---

There is old wisdom in choosing the lower seat. Not fighting for the head of the table. Not needing to be the most important person in the room.

The ego always wants the higher seat, the recognition, the deference, the spotlight. And chasing it keeps you tense, always measuring where you rank, always a little threatened by anyone who might outrank you.

Taking the lower seat on purpose is a strange relief. You stop competing for position. You let others be first. And oddly, you often end up more respected for it, because people trust someone who is not grasping for status.

This is not about thinking less of yourself. It is about thinking of yourself less in the moment, and letting the need to be elevated quietly fall away. The lower seat is more comfortable than it looks. You can see the whole room from there, and nobody can knock you down from it.
