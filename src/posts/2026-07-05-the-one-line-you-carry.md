date: 2026-07-05

---

Of all the tools, the simplest is a single line you carry. One sentence, ready for the moment the heat rises.

When you are flooded, complex ideas are useless. You cannot think through a whole framework. But you can hold one line. This will pass. Or, does this deserve my peace? Or, respond, do not react. Or, the reaction is short, the cost is long. Whichever one lands for you.

The one line works because it fits in the gap. In the hot second, when everything wants to react, a single true sentence is enough to buy the pause where a better choice can form.

So choose your line. Pick the one that steadies you, and keep it close, on a card, in your phone, in your memory. Then, when the moment comes, you will have something to grab. Not a lecture, not a method. One true thing, small enough to hold when you are flooded, and strong enough to keep you from reacting.
